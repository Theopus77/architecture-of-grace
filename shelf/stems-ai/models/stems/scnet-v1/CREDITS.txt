Split a song into its parts, Best quality (AOG-STEMS-AI-V1, aog-stems-ai.js)

The model: SCNet, "Sparse Compression Network for Music Source Separation"
  Weiming Tong, Jiaxin Zhu, Jie Chen, Shuai Kang, Tao Jiang, Yang Li, Zhiqiang Wei, Siwei Liu (ICASSP 2024).
  Code and weights: github.com/starrytong/SCNet, MIT License (copied below), Copyright (c) 2024 starrytong.
  The checkpoint: the author's SCNet trained on MUSDB18-HQ (10.1 million weights), as hosted with its config in
  github.com/ZFTurbo/Music-Source-Separation-Training, release v.1.0.6, file scnet_checkpoint_musdb18.ckpt
  (SHA-256 1bc0d1abb20bfdf966dcd07637bafd03e4bc13653d09ef18bc9b3e342eafe2aa). That repository is MIT License,
  Copyright (c) 2024 Roman Solovyev (ZFTurbo); its model code (models/scnet, commit 84b1eac0) is what these graphs
  follow. The SCNet repository, which links these weights, is MIT; @audio/neural-separate (MIT), which ships the same
  weights converted to ONNX, records that the author confirmed the weights are MIT and may be redistributed converted
  (github.com/starrytong/SCNet/issues/35).

What is here, and how it was made (tools/stems-model/ in the repository, not published):
  s0.onnx … s9.onnx   the network between its STFT and its iSTFT, written straight from the checkpoint into ten small
                      ONNX graphs (encoder, the six dual-path layers, three decoder levels), so a phone's single-threaded
                      onnxruntime keeps only one stage's memory at a time. Only drums, bass and vocals are made (the
                      page's "The rest" is the song less those three). Every change to the arithmetic is exact (the
                      decoder's repeated input folded into its weights; long convolutions cut into bands of rows).
  weights.bin         the 10.1 million weights in 8 bits with a scale per row (15 of them in 16 bits), 11.6 MB; the
                      page turns them back into 32-bit numbers as it loads each stage. Against the 32-bit weights the
                      parts come out 34 to 42 dB the same.
  manifest.json       where each weight sits.
