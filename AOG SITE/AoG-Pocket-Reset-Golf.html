Architecture of Grace
The Next Shot
for the walk between shots

The last one is already behind you.

A bladed chip, a three-putt, a drive into the trees — it happens to everyone who ever played. The walk to the next ball is yours. Take it.

Keep it on your home screen — open it right after you log the hole.

First · Name the lie

What’s the club in your hand right now?

Name it and it loosens its grip. Tap what’s true — or walk on.

Second · The pre-shot breath

Breathe down the fairway.

Longer out than in — the same breath the best players take over a putt. Let the ball settle.

Address the ball…
Third · Reset the body

Shake the tension out.

01

Regrip

Open the hands, roll the shoulders, ease the jaw. Tension lives in the grip.

02

Look up

Lift your eyes to the horizon. Name five things down the hole. You’re back in the round.

03

Walk it

Match your breath to your steps to the next ball. Arrive as a fresh player.

Fourth · The caddie, not the critic

Answer the voice in your head.

The voice after a bad shot isn’t your talent talking — it’s just noise. Hear your caddie instead.

The critic

“You always fall apart. You’ll never break through.”

The caddie

“One bad swing isn’t the round. Commit to this next one.”

You cooled it · that’s the game inside the game

Play the next shot — never the last one.
