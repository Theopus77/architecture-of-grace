New to all this? Here’s what it is, in plain words — and where to begin.

Architecture of Grace is a relationship-centered way of helping people grow — it begins with understanding someone before measuring them. It looks a little different depending on who you are, so pick the door that fits.

For Parents & Caregivers

Help your child feel understood

It’s a free, 5-minute, private check-in that helps your child reflect on how they’re really doing — not a test, not a grade. They answer honestly, and it offers gentle conversation starters you can use together. Nothing is shared without you.

Take the check-in →
For Educators

Know your students sooner

One honest answer from a student points you straight to the right lesson, story, or conversation — and the dashboard reminds you to tend to yourself first. No student logins, no grading, no surveillance. Just a clearer, kinder read on your class.

Open the dashboard →
For Administrators

See the whole picture, safely

You get de-identified, class- and school-level trends for MTSS — never individual scores. Privacy lives in the link, and there’s no data harvesting. It’s secular, trauma-informed, and built by a practicing special educator.
