For the teacher

<b>Three tiers, honestly counted.</b> Foundation is matching with a word bank, On-Level is multiple choice, Challenge is explain-and-apply against a model answer. Right on the first tap counts as independent; right after the explain-and-retry counts as supported — both are real evidence and neither is a grade.

<b>Send a link, get a row back.</b> Share a unit’s address from your dashboard link (or show it on the board). When the page can reach your Sheet, a Send button appears after a finished quiz — one tap posts one row to the <b>Practice</b> tab: unit, tier, independent, supported, hints, confidence, and the first name the student types. Nothing else ever travels, and a bare link stays fully private.

<b>The print button is the paper version.</b> Each unit prints its study pages and a blank quiz for the module on screen; the answer key prints only from the teacher drawer inside the unit.

How the set fits together

Every unit follows the same three-part pattern at the same three levels, and every unit carries a jump menu to all the others — so a student can move from cells to grammar without coming back here. Science card content is bilingual; answer choices stay in English on purpose, because those are the exam’s own words.
