Our song, in order (44 bars)

  1. Intro: keys alone (bars 1–4)
  2. The bass comes in (bars 5–8)
  3. The drums drop in: the groove (bars 9–24)
  4. The drop: drums and bass go away (bars 25–28)
  5. Everything back: the biggest part (bars 29–44)

The record goes the same way, with one more drop and a part with new notes in the middle. Add them later if you like.

1Set the song

95 beats a minute, a head-nod speed. The home note is E♭, and the mood is minor: dark and cool.

2The keys

Piano · the whole song

The keys play the loop from start to end. They keep the time for every part after them.

Open the Piano

The Piano may spell them with sharps: D♯m and A♯. Same notes, other names.

Why chord 7? In a minor key, the six pads give B♭ minor. The record uses the brighter B♭ major, which pulls you back home. The strip has it.

A drop is a hole in the music. Cut the drums and the bass at bars 25 and 29, then take out the piece between.
