Healthy Ways Around the World

World Health

People all over the world care for their health. In China, people do tai chi in parks. In India, people stretch with yoga. Families everywhere share warm soup and rest.

You can help keep yourself safe too. You can wear a helmet. You can buckle up. You can learn stop, drop and roll. You can find a safe adult.

In this unit, you will learn healthy ways from around the world. You will learn how to stay safe each day. You will know who to ask for help.

The big question

How do people around the world stay well and safe?

When it happened
Step 1

Step 2

Step 3

Buckle up in the back seat for the ride to school.

Step 4

Hold a grown-up's hand and cross at the crosswalk.

Step 5

Put on sunscreen and a hat before playing outside.

Step 6

Step 7

Eat a warm dinner with family, maybe with spices.

Step 8

Practice the fire escape plan and your address.

The story

Slow Moves in the Park

Nai Nai shows Leo a calm way to start the day.

It is early morning in a Chicago park. The grass is wet. Leo holds his grandmother's hand. He calls her Nai Nai. That means grandma in Chinese.

A group of people stand under the trees. They move their arms very slowly. They step softly, like cats. Nai Nai says, "This is tai chi."

Leo tries it. He lifts his arms like a bird. He breathes in and out. He wobbles, then he stands tall. Nai Nai smiles at him.

"My grandma taught me this in China," Nai Nai says. "Now I teach you." Leo feels calm and warm. He wants to come back tomorrow.

Talk about itWhat does your family do to feel calm or stay well?

People Everywhere Care

Main ideaPeople all over the world have cared for their health for a very long time.

Long, long ago, people got sick, just like today. They got scrapes and coughs. They wanted to feel better.

Tea and Tai Chi

Main ideaIn China, many people drink tea and do tai chi, slow gentle moves.

In China, many people drink tea. Tea is made from leaves and hot water. Families sip tea together.

Many people also do in parks. Tai chi is slow, gentle moving. People breathe calmly as they move.

Scientists have studied tai chi. It can help people keep their balance. Older people often like it.

Staying Safe

Safety
Big questionWhat can I do to keep myself safe every day?
The story

Rosa Leads the Way

A family practices getting out of the house, and the youngest shows the way.

Beep, beep, beep! Mom pushes the smoke alarm button. "This is only practice," she says. "Let's try our fire escape plan."

Rosa is the youngest. She is six. She knows the plan. "Get low and go!" she calls. She crawls to the front door. Her big brother follows.

Rosa does not stop for her toys. She goes straight outside. She walks to the big tree by the sidewalk. That is the meeting spot.

Everyone is at the tree. Dad counts: one, two, three, four. "Rosa, you led the way!" he says. Rosa grins. She knows just what to do.

6.6

Fire Safety

Main ideaIf your clothes catch fire, stop, drop and roll.

A beeps loudly when there is smoke. When you hear it, get out fast. Go to your meeting spot.

If your clothes catch fire, do not run. Stop where you are. Drop to the ground. Cover your face and roll.

Rolling puts out the flames. Practice it with your family. Then you will know what to do.

From the source
Fire Safety Steps
If your clothes catch fire, stop, drop to the ground, cover your face with your hands, and roll over and over until the fire is out.
National Fire Protection Association, Stop, Drop and Roll safety tip sheet (paraphrased)
