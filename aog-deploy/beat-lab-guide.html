Make a killer song on the Beat Lab

Eight steps, from a beat to a whole song. Every picture is the real Beat Lab.

★Start here · the Beat Lab

Four banks of sixteen pads. Each bank has one job.

BankA drums · B chords · C notes · D chops of a record.
The padsTap one. The middle is loud, the edge is soft.
Play and recordRecord the loop keeps what you tap. Undo takes it back.
The feelHow fast, how long, and how much it bounces.
HelpersRepeat plays a held pad in time. Click counts the beats.
Keep itYour loop as one file, with no gap.

1Pick a beat

Bank A. There are 105 beats to start from.

  1. Open Start from a beat. Pick one.
  2. Press ▶ Play.
  3. Does your head nod? Keep it. If not, pick another.

Try this: Boom bap · 90 and Lo-fi study beat are great first beats.

2Make the beat yours

Change one or two hits. Not more.

  1. Tap a pad to pick it. Its steps show below.
  2. Tap a step to add a hit. Tap it again to take it away.
  3. Listen. Keep what makes it better.

Try this: Turn on Record the loop. Tap the snare near its edge, just before beat 2. A soft “ghost” hit makes it groove.

3Set the feel

Tempo, length and swing.

  1. Tempo: 85 to 95 for hip-hop. 120 to 128 for dance.
  2. Loop length: 4 bars. That gives room for chords.
  3. Swing: 54 to 58 % makes it bounce.

4Add chords

Bank B. Four chords, one a bar.

  1. Pick Bank B. Pick an instrument and a key.
  2. Turn on Record the loop.
  3. On the first beat of each bar, tap pads 1, 5, 6, 4.
  4. Hold each pad for the whole bar.

Try this: Those are the chords of a thousand hit songs. For a sad song, pick Minor.

5Add the bass

Bank C. Same key as the chords.

  1. Pick Bank C. Set Key and Scale the same as bank B (C, Major).
  2. Now pads 1, 5, 6, 4 are the chords' own low notes.
  3. Record them with the kick: same bars, same pads as the chords.

Try this: Kick and bass together hit hardest. Let the bass rest when the kick rests.

6Sprinkle chops

Bank D. A record, cut in sixteen.

  1. Pick Bank D. Pick a record.
  2. Tap the chops to find a sound you like.
  3. Record one in the gaps, near the end of bar 2 and bar 4.

Try this: A little goes a long way. One chop every two bars is plenty.

7Mix it

Each pad has its own volume, pitch and place.

  1. Kick and snare loudest. Hi-hats softer, about 70 %.
  2. Move the hi-hats a little left, the chops a little right.
  3. Pitch a chop down 3 to 5 steps for a deep, dark sound.

8Build the song

A song is your loop in parts: quiet, fuller, then everything.

  1. Chorus: with everything playing, press Make the loop into a file, then Send to the Mixing Desk.
  2. Verse: on bank D, Clear this pad's hits on the chops. Make a file and send it.
  3. Intro: clear the drums and the bass too, so only chords play. Make a file and send it.
  4. Undo brings every part back.
  5. On the Mixing Desk, put each part on its own track. Set where it Starts at bar and how many times it Plays.

⚡Five secrets of killer songs

  • Leave space.The quiet gaps are part of the groove.
  • Kick and bass together.They hit as one, so the song feels strong.
  • Soft and loud.Soft hits between loud ones make it move. Tap near the edge.
  • Change something every 4 or 8 bars.Add a sound, take one away, or add a fill.
  • Stop before the chorus.One beat of silence, then everything at once.
