The song with no voice, made by you. Five parts, one at a time. Tick each box as you go.

0The plan: 5 rounds

A round is the four chords, one time through: 16 beats. The song is 5 rounds. Each round, something new joins in.

Keep the recording close. Listen to a part, then play it. Your ears are the best teacher.

1Set the song

Runaway is slow (87 beats a minute) and its home note is E.

That switch is the secret: you hear what you already made while you record the next part, and every part lands in the right place.

4The drums

Drum Machine · rounds 3–5

On the record, the drums are a sample: a drum break from a 1971 funk record, chopped up. Here we play it ourselves.

Tap the filled squares. Orange ones are the loud hits. Then listen to the recording and fix any square that sounds different.
