1Set the song

Runaway is slow (87 beats a minute) and its home note is E.

That switch is the secret: you hear what you already made while you record the next part, and every part lands in the right place.

2The guide click

Drum Machine · all 5 rounds

A steady tick that every part follows. Studios call it a click track. You turn it off at the end.

Open the Drum Machine

3The piano riff

Piano · all 5 rounds

One note every 2 beats. Count 1 2 3 4 and play on 1 and 3.

High E: the E two octaves above middle C. Low E: the E one octave below it.

Round 1: the high E, 16 times.

Rounds 2–5: four groups of four notes. Each group is 2 bars.

Group 1
E E E
+ low E
Group 2
D♯ D♯ D♯
+ low D♯
Group 3
C♯ C♯ C♯
+ low C♯
Group 4
A A G♯
+ high E

The fourth note drops an octave, except in group 4: there it jumps back up to the high E. A and G♯ sit just below the high C♯.

Open the Piano

A note out of place? Record it again.

In the Piano

First check the pads read E, F♯m, G♯m, A, B, C♯m. If not, press the Set button in part 1, then come back.

No C♯m on your screen? In Any chord, tap D♭, then Minor. D♭ minor is the same chord as C♯ minor.
