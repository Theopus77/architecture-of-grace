The Oscilloscope

A glowing line that shows a sound. Change it, hear it, and test one idea at a time.

What the line shows

You seeIt means
Waves close togetherA fast wiggle — a high sound (high pitch).
Waves far apartA slow wiggle — a low sound.
A tall lineA loud sound (big amplitude).
A short lineA quiet sound.
A smooth, square, triangle or saw shapeThe same note with a different "colour" of sound.

The six rooms

  • Bench — the moving wave. Start here.
  • Meet — tap the crest, the trough, the height and the width to name them.
  • Play — turn the two knobs and pick a shape. Press Hear only if you want sound.
  • Lab — run a fair test (below).
  • Find — match the wave you are asked for.
  • Job — a short task to finish and share.
  • Lessons — seventeen lessons, one step at a time, each with a worksheet.

The lessons

Seventeen short lessons live in the Lessons room. Each one is a few moves on the real controls; a check appears when the scope sees you did it. Every lesson has a worksheet you can type on or print: the lesson worksheets.

A fair test in four steps

  1. Pick a question, like "Does a taller wave sound louder?"
  2. Change one thing. Keep everything else the same.
  3. Run it three times.
  4. Write what the data say — one sentence is enough.
Kind to the roomKeep the volume low, or leave sound off. Everything works silently. Nothing is saved or sent.
