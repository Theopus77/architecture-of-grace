Social Studies · Activity 2

Because of That

Every event in history is somebody's because. Find what each cause set in motion — all four choices really happened, but only one grew out of this one.

Show me one first

This one is nobody's answer — it is only here to show the moves before you touch yours. Watching it is instruction, never a trial, and it counts toward nothing.

Cause: A machine is invented that pulls a train faster than any horse can run.
Read all four effects. Every one really happened somewhere — that is what makes this honest work.
Test each: could it happen WITHOUT the fast machine? Cities grew for many reasons — but tracks spreading across the map needed exactly this.
Railroads spread — and towns grew wherever the tracks went. The effect that NEEDS the cause is the one.

Now number 1 is yours. The hint button is there the whole time, and using it is part of the lesson.

Question 1 of 8 One cause, one true effect.

Need help?

For each cause, write what happened because of it. One sentence is enough — the word because should fit inside it.

  1. 1Cause: The Articles of Confederation left the government too weak to tax or keep order. — what happened because of it?
  2. 2Cause: Eli Whitney's cotton gin cleaned cotton about fifty times faster than hands could. — what happened because of it?
  3. 3Cause: The United States bought the Louisiana Territory from France in 1803. — what happened because of it?
  4. 4Cause: Congress raised the tariff — the tax on goods brought in from other countries. — what happened because of it?
  5. 5Cause: Lincoln won the election of 1860 without a single Southern electoral vote. — what happened because of it?
  6. 6Cause: Cotton and slavery had spread across the Deep South, until the region's whole economy stood on them. — what did it cause next?
  7. 7Cause: Seven Southern states had declared they were leaving the Union. — what did it cause next?
  8. 8Cause: California — crowded overnight by the gold rush — asked to enter the Union as a free state. — what did it cause next?
How confident are you with this skill?

There is no right answer here. It only tells your teacher where to start next time.

Done

Eight becauses, found.

The three you did not pick were never junk — each was the true effect of a different cause. History is a room full of chains, and you just learned to follow one without grabbing the others.
