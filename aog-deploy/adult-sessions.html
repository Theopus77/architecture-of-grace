Crisis & safety resources

National baseline — post these alongside your local mobile-crisis line and your institution’s emergency contacts.

  • 988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline — call or text 988. 24/7, free, confidential.
  • Crisis Text Line — text HOME to 741741.
  • SAMHSA National Helpline — 1-800-662-4357. Free, confidential treatment referral and information, 24/7.
  • Veterans Crisis Line — dial 988, then press 1 (or text 838255).
  • Immediate danger — call 911 or your local emergency number.
