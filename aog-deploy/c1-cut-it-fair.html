Cut It Fair

One whole. Cut it so every part is the same size — a fair share. Every fraction starts right here.

Watching one first is a strong way to start.

Words for it

Fair shares — parts that are all the same size. Nobody's piece is bigger.

Fractional parts — the equal parts one whole is cut into. Fractions are made of them.
