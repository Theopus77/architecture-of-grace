Which One Doesn't Belong

Finding the odd one is the easy half. Saying what the other three share is the half that turns a list into a map.

Show me one first

This one is nobody's word — it is only here to show the moves before you touch yours. Watching it costs nothing and is written down nowhere.

chlorophyll · chloroplasts · glucose · microbes
Three of them are only ever found in a plant.
Microbes live on their own, almost anywhere.
So: microbes — and the group is inside a green plant.

Now word 1 is yours. The hint button is there the whole time, and using it is part of the lesson.
