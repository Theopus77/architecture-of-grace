    How musicians learn a tune by ear
    1. Loop one short phrase: two or three seconds.
    2. Slow it to 60 or 70 %. Hum along until you can hum it alone.
    3. Pause on the first note. Press What notes are these? and find the one your voice matches.
    4. Play it in the Studio. Then the next note: up or down, a step or a jump?
    5. When the phrase is right, speed back up to 100 % and play along.

    Keys on a computer: Space plays and pauses, [ and ] set the loop, L turns the loop on and off, ← and → jump a second.
