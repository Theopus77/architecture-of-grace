The course follows the arc of a standard junior-high U.S. history textbook — units, chapters, sections, numbered lessons — in original text written at a grade 6–8 reading level. Every lesson has a main idea, a three- or four-paragraph reading with key words you can tap, a source, a data table or a scenario to think through, and three checks.
