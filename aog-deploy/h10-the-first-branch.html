Social Studies · Activity 10

The First Branch

Article I comes first because the founders expected this branch to lead: two rooms, one pen, and the purse. The two rooms below are live — play them, tap them, watch a bill walk. Then three sets: the basics, the road a bill travels, and the powers at work in this era.

Before the questions — watch it move

The two rooms at a glance

Ten stops — play them like a film, or tap through. The picture moves: two rooms light up, a bill walks its road, the purse and the era's bargains take the stage. None of it is a test. Every stop ends at the set of questions where that idea is practiced for real.

Show me one first

This one is nobody's answer — it is only here to show the moves before you touch yours. Watching it is instruction, never a trial, and it counts toward nothing.

A town wants a new speed-limit law. Who can actually write it into law — the mayor's police, a judge, or the town council?
Sort the jobs: police ENFORCE — that is executive work. A judge INTERPRETS — judicial work. Neither holds the pen.
The council — the town's little legislature. The pen always belongs to the branch that represents; scale it up and you have Congress.

Now number 1 is yours. The hint button is there the whole time, and using it is part of the lesson.

Need help?

Answer in your own words. At the bottom, draw the road a bill travels: committee → both houses → the president's desk — and mark where a veto can turn it back.

  1. 1Which branch writes the nation's laws?
  2. 2Congress has two houses. What are they called?
  3. 3In which house does a state's number of seats depend on its population?
  4. 4How many senators does each state send, and for how long?
  5. 5Besides writing laws, which of these powers belongs to Congress?
  6. 6The president vetoes a bill Congress passed. What can Congress do?
  7. 7A president signs a treaty and nominates a judge. Who must say yes before either counts?
  8. 8A president or judge is accused of serious misconduct. Which branch can remove them — and how?
