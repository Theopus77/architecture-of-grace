Concepts & Data · Activity 13

Trade a Ten

Work one column at a time, ones first. Take away what you can — and when the top digit is too small, trade a ten from next door. Every trade gets written, just like a pencil would.

Watching one first is a strong way to start.

Problem 1 of 8 Take what you can. Trade when you can’t.

You traded your way through all eight.

A ten is just ten ones wearing a coat. When a column comes up short, the neighbor lends a ten and nothing is lost — the amount stays the same, written a friendlier way. That one idea carries subtraction as far as you will ever need it to go.
