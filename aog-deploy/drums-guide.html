The Drum Machine, in pictures

Almost no reading. Tape it to the wall next to the machine. The first page is the Simple bench; the rest is the Full bench.

★Start here · the Simple bench

This is what the machine shows first. Everything you need for a beat is on it. Full bench brings back every switch on the real SP-1200 — that is what the rest of this guide shows.

Simple bench · Full benchTap to swap. The machine remembers which one you like.
The three buttonsOrange plays and stops. Red clears the beat. Blue sends it to the turntables. On the Full bench these are RUN, CLEAR and TO THE DECKS.
Sounds · PartA and B are the classic kit. C is electro: boom, snap, cowbell, conga, zap. D is dusty breaks: thump, crack, shaker, tambourine, snap, timbale, woodblock, triangle. Part 1 to 8 are the eight segments of your song.
The padsTap one to hear it. Kick, snare, hats, clap, tom, rim, bell.
The gridSixteen boxes for each sound. Lit = it plays. On a phone, slide it sideways.
Tempo · Room volumeFaster or slower. Louder or quieter.
Starter patternsTap one and a beat is already there. Change it.

1What everything is

The screenWhich bank, the tempo, the swing, and whether it is running.
RUNStarts it. Press it again to stop.
SWINGStraight, or lazy. Six steps.
TO THE DECKSSends your beat over to the turntables.
SELECTFour sets of sounds. A and B are full. C and D are empty and yours.
The sliders and the eight padsSlider = how loud that sound is. Pad = tap it to hear it. One row of eight, numbered 1 to 8.
The gridSixteen boxes for each sound. Lit = it plays. The big numbers are the beats you count.

2Your first beat

Four moves. That is the whole thing.

1 Press RUN A light starts walking across the grid.
2 Light up the KICK row Click the boxes under the big numbers.
3 Now the snare Tap the pad to hear it. Then light up beats 2 and 4.
4 Drag TEMPO It is under the machine. Until it feels right in your chest.

3One box, four taps

Keep clicking the same box. It goes round.

So to clear a box you keep tapping until it goes dark again.

5Five beats to copy

6Swing

The SWING button up top. Slides the in-between notes late. Straight on the left, lazy on the right. Six steps — the real machine had six.

9Nothing is happening

  • No sound at allRUN is not pressed. Press it once.
  • One pad is silentIts slider is at the bottom. Push it up.
  • Whole bank is silentSELECT is on C or D. Those start empty. Press A.
  • It sounds like a messToo fast. Drag TEMPO down and take one sound back out.
  • I want a box goneKeep tapping it. On, louder, green, then off.
