1What everything is

The screenWhich kit, the tempo, the swing, and whether it is running.
RUNStarts it. Press it again to stop.
SWINGStraight, or lazy. Six steps.
TO THE DECKSSends your beat over to the turntables.
SOUNDSTwenty-five kits, A to Y. The screen shows the letter.
REPEATSame as Roll. Hold a pad and it repeats.
The sliders and the eight padsSlider = how loud that sound is. Pad = tap it to hear it. One row of eight, numbered 1 to 8.
The gridSixteen boxes for each sound. Lit = it plays. The big numbers are the beats you count.

2Your first beat

Four moves. That is the whole thing.

1 Press RUN A light starts walking across the grid.
2 Light up the KICK row Click the boxes under the big numbers.
3 Now the snare Tap the pad to hear it. Then light up beats 2 and 4.
4 Drag TEMPO It is under the machine. Until it feels right in your chest.

3Four more things to try

On both benches.

Start from a beat Pick a style. Its kit, tempo and swing come too. Want yours back? Press Put my beat back.
Slide the Sound dial Left is 1987 crunch. Right is clean 2026.
Roll Tap: 1/8, 1/16, 1/32, off. Hold a pad. It repeats in time. With REC on, it records.
On a computer Keys A S D F and J K L ; play the pads. Space starts and stops.

Two fingers can play two pads at once.

4One box, four taps

Keep clicking the same box. It goes round.

So to clear a box you keep tapping until it goes dark again.

6Five beats to copy

Light up the same boxes and press RUN. Or pick the one with the same name.

7Swing

The SWING button up top. Slides the in-between notes late. Straight on the left, lazy on the right. Six steps — the real machine had six.

Kits A, B, C, F and L are drum-machine sounds. The other twenty are recordings of real drums and percussion. A recorded kit loads the first time you pick it. An accent is a harder hit. On some kits it is also a new sound: the snare cracks on its rim, the ride rings its bell.

Your own sound on a pad plays instead of the kit sound. Want the kit back? Next to Sounds, press Put the kit sounds back. Changed your mind? Press Put my sounds back.

10Something is not right

  • No sound at allRUN is not pressed. Press it once.
  • One pad is silentIts slider is at the bottom. Push it up.
  • A pad plays the wrong soundYour own sound is on it. Next to Sounds, press Put the kit sounds back.
  • A pad keeps hittingRoll is on. Tap it until it says off.
  • My beat is goneA new beat took its place. Press Put my beat back.
  • It sounds like a messToo fast. Drag TEMPO down and take one sound back out.
  • I want a box goneKeep tapping it. On, louder, green, then off.
