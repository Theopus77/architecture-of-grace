Concepts & Data · Activity 10

Stems and Leaves

Every two-digit score splits in two: the tens digit is the stem, the ones digit is the leaf. Put each leaf on its stem, and the whole pile of numbers lines itself up.

A stem-and-leaf plot is the data itself, standing in order. No score disappears into a bar or an average — so the biggest, the count, and the middle are all just sitting there, waiting to be read.
