Three years in Room 36

Grade 6 is Year A. Grade 7 is Year B. Grade 8 is Year C. A student can have Room 36 three years running and never repeat a year.

How the three years work

  • AYear A · Grade 6The lesson as written.
  • BYear B · Grade 7A new story and a different part of the novel.
  • CYear C · Grade 8A leading role and a new story.
  • Every year keeps each lesson’s goal, standards and safety steps.
  • No year raises a lesson’s risk. The ★★ lessons keep their whole protocol every year.
  • Year B stories happen at Lincoln Park, the novel’s school. They are new; they do not retell the book.
  • New to Room 36 in grade 7 or 8? Use that grade’s year. Every year teaches every idea.
Year C: rules for leading
  • Leaders use made-up examples only.
  • Younger students never share personal stories. Leaders never read anyone’s private writing.
  • An adult stays in the room: you, or the younger class’s teacher.
  • Leaders are not counselors. If anything worries them, they tell an adult right away.
  • An adult reads anything made for the school (a poster, a guide, a card) before it goes up.
  • In the ★ and ★★ lessons, Year C makes something for the school or helps plan a calm part of the lesson. It never leads the private work.
If a student tells you something serious
  • 988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline: call or text 988. Crisis Text Line: text HOME to 741741.
  • Every school employee in Illinois is a mandated reporter. If you have reasonable cause to believe a child is abused or neglected, call the DCFS Hotline yourself: 1-800-25-ABUSE (1-800-252-2873). Do not investigate first, and do not hand the call to someone else.
