For the teacher

The course reads the Bible as literature and history in a public school: its stories for the youngest readers, its big story in the middle grades, its genres and its world in 6–8, and close reading and interpretation in high school — as units, chapters, sections and numbered lessons, in original text written at each band’s reading level. Beliefs are always attributed (“Christians believe…”, “Jewish tradition reads…”), the text is quoted from public-domain translations or paraphrased in plain words, and every lesson ends with three checks answerable from its reading.

Trusted sources to read alongside the course

  • The King James Version and the World English Bible: public-domain translations.
  • The New Revised Standard Version and The New Oxford Annotated Bible: the standard study edition.
  • Robert Alter, The Art of Biblical Narrative: how the stories are told.
  • Bible Literacy Project, The Bible and Its Influence: a textbook written for public schools.
  • American Academy of Religion, Guidelines for Teaching About Religion in K–12 Public Schools.
