Every living thing is made of cells

A leaf. Smooth and green — nothing on it looks divided into parts.

Everything alive is made of cells — you are built from roughly thirty trillion of them. The nucleus holds the DNA and directs the cell; the membrane decides what gets in and out; and the organelles inside each do one job.

What people get wrong

⚠️ People often think…

Plants do photosynthesis, so they do not do cellular respiration.

They do both, all the time. Photosynthesis stores energy in sugar; respiration is what releases it as ATP, and every plant cell needs ATP day and night. One fills the store, the other spends from it.

Plants do both. Every cell needs ATP.

⚠️ People often think…

Cellular respiration is a science word for breathing.

Breathing moves air into and out of lungs. Cellular respiration happens inside every cell, in the mitochondria, breaking sugar down to release energy. Breathing supplies one ingredient; it is not the same process.

Lungs move air. Cells break down sugar.

⚠️ People often think…

Animal cells have mitochondria; plant cells have chloroplasts instead.

Plant cells have BOTH. They need chloroplasts to make sugar and mitochondria to spend it. What animal cells lack is chloroplasts — and a cell wall, and a large central vacuole.

Plant cells have both. Animal cells lack chloroplasts.

⚠️ People often think…

Osmosis and diffusion are two words for the same thing.

Diffusion is any particle spreading from crowded to less crowded. Osmosis is specifically WATER crossing a membrane toward the side with more dissolved material. Osmosis is one case of diffusion, not a synonym.

Osmosis is water across a membrane.

Find the part, name the job

1 Watch one

A plant is kept in the dark for a week. What happens inside its cells?

  1. No light means the chloroplasts stop making sugar.
  2. But the mitochondria keep running — every cell still needs ATP.
  3. So the plant keeps spending stored sugar with nothing refilling it.
  4. The store runs out and the plant starves. Both processes were running the whole time. ✓

Helpful Hints

🧱 Cell theory — three sentences, easy points

1. All living things are made of cells. 2. The cell is the basic unit of life. 3. All cells come from existing cells.

All living · Basic unit · Cells from cells

🔁 The two big equations are mirror images

Photosynthesis: CO₂ + water + light → sugar + oxygen. Cellular respiration: sugar + oxygen → CO₂ + water + energy. One builds sugar, the other burns it.

Flip the arrow.

⚠️ Traps the test loves

  • Plants DO cellular respiration — every living cell needs to release energy.
  • Cellular respiration ≠ breathing. It happens inside cells, in mitochondria.
  • Both plant AND animal cells have mitochondria.
  • Osmosis is only about water — diffusion is the general word.
  • Cells divide instead of growing forever — a giant cell can’t move materials fast enough.

🏭 Organelle jobs — think of a factory

  • Nucleus = the main office (holds DNA).
  • Mitochondria = power plant (releases energy).
  • Chloroplast = solar panel + kitchen (plants only).
  • Cell membrane = security gate (controls what enters/exits).
  • Cell wall = stiff outer wall (plants only).

🎯 How the test will ask

  • Expect a labeled cell diagram — know organelles by shape and location.
  • Mitosis questions love ORDER: PMAT — Please Make A Taco.
  • Transport hinges on one word: passive = no energy, active = energy needed.

✅ Before the test, can you…

  • Say all three parts of cell theory?
  • Name 3 differences between plant and animal cells?
  • Put PMAT in order and say what each phase does?
  • Write both equations (photosynthesis and respiration)?
  • Explain WHY cells divide instead of growing huge?
