A focus is not the center

An ellipse. It has a center, like any oval — and it also has two special points called foci.

The Sun sits at a FOCUS of the ellipse, not at the center. That is why a planet has a near end and a far end to its orbit — and it moves fastest at the near end, slowest at the far one.

What people get wrong

⚠️ People often think…

The Sun is burning, the way a fire burns.

Burning is a chemical reaction and would have used the Sun up in a few thousand years. The Sun runs on FUSION — hydrogen nuclei pressed into helium — which releases millions of times more energy per kilogram. It is not fission either.

Fusion: hydrogen into helium.

⚠️ People often think…

Earth’s core is solid all the way through.

The OUTER core is liquid iron, and its motion is what generates the magnetic field. The INNER core is solid — hotter still, but held solid by immense pressure. Two layers, two states.

Outer liquid, inner solid under pressure.

⚠️ People often think…

All the elements were made in the Big Bang.

It produced essentially only hydrogen and helium. Everything heavier — the carbon in you, the iron in your blood, the oxygen you breathe — was fused inside stars and scattered when they died.

Hydrogen and helium first. Stars made the rest.

⚠️ People often think…

One brutally cold winter settles an argument about climate.

One season is weather. Climate is a thirty-year average, and a single extreme year sits inside the noise of any long record. The same rule cuts both ways — one hot summer does not settle it either.

Thirty years, not one season.

Why does a comet move so fast near the Sun and so slowly far out?

  1. Its orbit is a long ellipse with the Sun at one focus.
  2. Near the Sun, gravity is far stronger, so it is pulled hard.
  3. Far out, gravity has weakened and it coasts.
  4. Fastest at the near end, slowest at the far end — which is why comets appear briefly and vanish for decades. ✓

Helpful Hints

🪜 Earth and space — the ladder

The universe began 13.8 billion years ago and is still expanding — redshift says so → stars fuse hydrogen into helium and, in their deaths, into everything heavier → our Sun and planets condensed from that debris 4.6 billion years ago → gravity holds the planets in ellipses; Kepler’s laws give the speeds and the years → Earth’s inner heat sorted it into crust, mantle, outer core, inner core, and drives the plates → greenhouse gases keep the surface about 33 °C warmer than bare rock; feedbacks amplify or dampen any change → CO₂ 280 → 420 ppm, +1.2 °C, no carbon-14 in the new carbon, a cooling upper atmosphere: the warming is measured, fast, and ours.

Expanding, fused, condensed, orbiting, layered, blanketed, measured.

🤝 The whole idea

Every claim in this unit rests on something measured — a redshift, a spectrum, a seismic shadow, a gas reading on a mountain. The story is enormous, but it is built from instruments, not opinions.

Big story, small instruments.

⚠️ Tricky ones

  • The Sun does FUSION (hydrogen → helium), not fission and not burning.
  • The Big Bang made only hydrogen and helium. Every atom heavier was made in a STAR.
  • The Sun sits at a FOCUS of the ellipse, not the center — and planets speed up when closest.
  • The OUTER core is liquid (magnetic field); the INNER core is solid, kept solid by pressure.
  • One cold winter is WEATHER. A thirty-year average is CLIMATE. Don’t argue trends with single days.
  • “The climate changed before” is TRUE — and it proves CO₂ and temperature are coupled. The new thing is the RATE.
  • Volcanoes emit about 1% of what humans do. The Sun has been flat since the 1980s.

🎯 How the test will ask

  • “What evidence supports the Big Bang?” (redshift of galaxies, the cosmic microwave background, the hydrogen–helium ratio).
  • “Where did the carbon in your body come from?” (fused in stars, scattered by supernovae).
  • A cross-section of Earth with letters — “which layer generates the magnetic field, and why?”
  • CO₂ and temperature data — “describe the relationship and explain the mechanism.”
  • “Give one positive and one negative feedback in the climate system.”
  • “A planet is 9 times farther than Earth. How long is its year?” (9³ = 729; √729 = 27).
