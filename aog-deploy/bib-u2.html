Abraham's Family

Genesis Stories

This unit is about one family. It starts with Abraham and Sarah. It ends with Joseph in Egypt. Genesis, the first book of the Bible, tells these stories. Jews, Christians and Muslims all tell stories about this family.

You will meet a promise under the stars. You will meet twins who fought and made up. You will meet a boy with a special coat. And you will see what happens when he chooses to forgive.

By the end, you will be able to say who is in the family. You will draw a family tree. And you will be able to say what the story says a promise and forgiving can do.

The big question

What holds a family together in the stories of Genesis?

Abraham, Sarah and a Promise

Abraham
Big questionWhat does the story say a promise can do for a family?
The story

More Stars Than You Can Count

One night, an old man walks out of his tent and looks up.

It is night in the desert. A man named Abraham steps out of his tent. The air is cool. The sky is black and full of stars. He tips his head back and looks up.

Genesis tells this story. Abraham and his wife Sarah are old. They have no children. That makes them sad. The story says God tells Abraham to look at the sky.

Try to count the stars, the story says God tells him. Abraham looks. He counts one, two, three. Then ten. Then a hundred. There are too many to count.

The story says God makes a promise. Abraham's family will be as many as the stars. Abraham looks up one more time. Then he goes back into the tent to tell Sarah.

Talk about itHave you ever tried to count the stars? What did it feel like?
Section 1

Leaving Home

3.1

Abraham and Sarah Set Out

Main ideaThe story says Abraham and Sarah left home because God told them to go.

Genesis is the first book of the Bible. It tells about a man named Abraham. He lived in a city called Ur. Later his family moved to a place called Haran.

The story says God spoke to Abraham. God said, go to a new land. I will show you where. Abraham was not young. He was seventy-five years old.

Abraham packed up his tents. His wife Sarah came too. So did his nephew Lot. They walked a long way. The story calls the new land .

Think it through
Picture It

Look at a tent folded up on a camel's back. A bag of flour hangs beside it. An old man holds a walking stick. An old woman ties on her sandals. They do not know where they are going yet.

What would you pack if you had to walk to a new home?

Words to know
Genesis
the first book of the Bible; it tells the story of Abraham's family
Canaan
the name the story gives to the land Abraham and Sarah walked to
Check yourself

1. Which book of the Bible tells about Abraham?

Why: Genesis is the first book. It tells the story of Abraham and his family.

2. Why did Abraham leave home, in the story?

Why: The story says God spoke to Abraham and told him to go to a new land.

3. Who went with Abraham?

Why: Genesis says Sarah and Lot went with Abraham on the trip.
3.2

A Promise Under the Stars

Main ideaThe story says God promised Abraham a family as many as the stars.

A is when you say you will do something. Then you do it. The story says God made a big promise to Abraham.

God told Abraham to look at the night sky. Count the stars, if you can. Nobody can count them all. The story says, your family will be that many.

Abraham had no children yet. Still, the story says he believed the promise. This promise is very important to Jews, Christians and Muslims.

From the source
Look Up and Count
The story says God took Abraham outside at night. God said, look at the sky. Count the stars if you can. Your family will be that many.
Genesis 15, told in simple words from the King James Version (1611) (paraphrased)

Why do you think the story picks stars, and not rocks or trees?

Words to know
promise
saying you will do something, and then doing it
Check yourself

1. What is a promise?

Why: A promise means you say you will do something and then you do it.

2. What did the story say Abraham's family would be like?

Why: Genesis says God told Abraham his family would be as many as the stars.

3. How many children did Abraham have when he got the promise?

Why: Abraham and Sarah had no children yet when the story says God made the promise.
Section 2

A Baby and a Bride

3.3

Isaac Is Born

Main ideaThe story says Sarah had a baby, Isaac, when she and Abraham were very old.

Abraham and Sarah waited a long time. The story says three visitors came to their tent. One said Sarah would have a son. Sarah laughed. She was too old, she thought.

But the story says a baby was born. They named him . That name sounds like the word for laughing in Hebrew. Sarah said, now everyone will laugh with me.

Abraham also had a son named Ishmael with a woman named Hagar. Muslims tell stories about Abraham, Hagar and Ishmael too. Jews and Christians tell about Isaac most.

Think it through
Why Laugh?

Sarah hears a voice say she will have a baby. She is very old. Her hair is white. She laughs to herself behind the tent door. Later, she holds a baby boy and laughs again. It is a different laugh.

How is the first laugh different from the second laugh?

Words to know
Isaac
the son of Abraham and Sarah; his name sounds like laughing
Hebrew
the language most of the Old Testament was first written in
Check yourself

1. What was the name of Abraham and Sarah's son?

Why: Genesis says Abraham and Sarah named their son Isaac.

2. What does the name Isaac sound like in Hebrew?

Why: The name Isaac sounds like the Hebrew word for laughing. Sarah laughed.

3. Who tells stories about Abraham?

Why: All three traditions tell stories about Abraham. Muslims also tell of Hagar and Ishmael.
3.4

Rebekah at the Well

Main ideaThe story says a kind girl named Rebekah became Isaac's wife.

Isaac grew up. Abraham wanted a wife for him. The story says he sent a helper back to Haran. The helper stopped by a with his camels.

A girl came to get water. Her name was Rebekah. The helper asked for a drink. She gave him one. Then she gave water to all ten camels. That is a lot of water!

The helper knew she was kind. The story says Rebekah agreed to go. She married Isaac. Later they had twin boys.

Read the numbers
Water for the Camels

Genesis 24 and 25, King James Version (1611)

Ten camels drink a lot. What does that tell you about Rebekah?

Words to know
well
a deep hole in the ground where people get water
Check yourself

1. Where did the helper meet Rebekah?

Why: Genesis says the helper waited by a well, and Rebekah came to get water.

2. What did Rebekah do for the camels?

Why: The story says Rebekah gave water to all the camels. It showed she was kind.

3. Who did Rebekah marry?

Why: Rebekah married Isaac, the son of Abraham and Sarah.
Section 3

Twins and a Ladder

3.5

Jacob and Esau

Main ideaThe story says twins Jacob and Esau fought, and then made up.

Isaac and Rebekah had . Esau came out first. He was hairy and loved to hunt. Jacob came second. He liked to stay by the tents.

The twins did not get along. The story says Jacob tricked Esau two times. He got what was meant for the older brother. Esau was very angry. Jacob ran away.

Many years went by. The story says Jacob came home with gifts. Esau ran to meet him. He hugged him. They both cried. The brothers made up.

From the source

The story says Jacob was afraid to see Esau. He bowed down seven times. But Esau ran to him. Esau hugged him and kissed him. They both cried.
Genesis 33, told in simple words from the King James Version (1611) (paraphrased)

Jacob was scared. What did Esau do instead of being mean?

Words to know
twins
two children born to the same mother on the same day
Esau
Jacob's twin brother; he was born first and loved to hunt
Check yourself

1. Who were the twins?

Why: Genesis says Isaac and Rebekah's twin sons were Esau and Jacob.

2. Which twin was born first?

Why: The story says Esau was born first. Jacob came second.

3. How does the story of the twins end?

Why: Genesis 33 says Esau ran to Jacob, hugged him, and they made up.
3.6

A Ladder to the Sky

Main ideaThe story says Jacob dreamed of a ladder with angels going up and down.

Jacob was running away from Esau. Night came. He had no bed. He put a stone under his head and went to sleep.

The story says he had a . He saw a ladder. The bottom was on the ground. The top reached the sky. Angels went up and down on it.

In the dream, the story says God spoke. God gave Jacob the same promise as Abraham. Jacob woke up. He named the place Bethel.

Think it through
See the Ladder

Look at a boy asleep on the ground. A rock is his pillow. Above him a ladder goes up and up, into the stars. Angels step up the ladder. Other angels step down. Nobody is in a hurry.

Why do you think the angels go both up and down?

Words to know
dream
pictures and stories you see while you sleep
angel
in the Bible, a messenger sent from God
Check yourself

1. What did Jacob use for a pillow?

Why: Genesis says Jacob put a stone under his head to sleep.

2. What did Jacob see in his dream?

Why: The story says Jacob dreamed of a ladder to the sky with angels on it.

3. What did God tell Jacob in the dream, in the story?

Why: Genesis 28 says God repeated the promise made to Abraham.
3.7

A Family Tree

Main ideaThe story says Jacob had twelve sons, and you can draw the whole family as a tree.

Jacob got married. The story says he had two wives, Leah and Rachel. Jacob had twelve sons and a daughter named Dinah. That is a big family!

A is a drawing. It shows who is whose parent. Abraham and Sarah go at the top. Isaac is under them. Jacob is under Isaac.

The story says God gave Jacob a new name. The name was Israel. The twelve sons became twelve big families. Jews call them the twelve tribes of Israel.

Read the numbers
Count the Family

Genesis 16, 21, 25 and 35, King James Version (1611)

Which row has the biggest number? Whose family is that?

Words to know
family tree

Israel
the new name the story says God gave to Jacob
Check yourself

1. How many sons did Jacob have?

Why: Genesis says Jacob had twelve sons. They became the twelve tribes.

2. What is a family tree?

Why: A family tree is a drawing that shows who is whose parent and child.

3. What new name did the story say God gave Jacob?

Why: Genesis 35 says God named Jacob Israel.

Joseph and His Brothers

Joseph
Big questionWhat does the story say happens when someone chooses to forgive?
The story

The Coat Everyone Wanted

A father gives one son a special coat, and eleven brothers watch.

Jacob has twelve sons. He loves Joseph the most. Joseph is one of the youngest. Genesis tells this story. One day Jacob gives Joseph a coat. It is a special coat. Some Bibles say it has many colors. Some say it has long sleeves.

Joseph puts the coat on. He walks past his brothers. The coat is bright in the sun. The brothers look at each other. Nobody gave them a coat like that.

Joseph has dreams, too. He tells his brothers about them. In one dream, their bundles of wheat bow down to his bundle. The brothers do not like that dream at all.

The story says the brothers began to hate Joseph. They could not say one kind word to him. This is where the trouble starts. But it is not where the story ends.

Talk about itHow do you think the brothers felt when they saw the coat?
Section 1

The Coat and the Dreams

4.1

Joseph's Special Coat

Main ideaThe story says Jacob loved Joseph most and gave him a special coat.

Jacob had twelve sons. Joseph was next to the youngest. The story says Jacob loved Joseph more than the others. That was a problem.

Jacob gave Joseph a special . The King James Bible calls it a coat of many colors. Other Bibles call it a long coat with sleeves. Either way, it was fancy.

The brothers saw the coat. The story says they were jealous. means you want what someone else has. They stopped being nice to Joseph.

Think it through
One Coat, Eleven Brothers

Look at twelve boys in a field. Eleven wear plain brown coats. One wears a coat that is bright and long. His father pats him on the back. The other boys stand with their arms crossed.

If you were one of the eleven, what would you want to say to your father?

Words to know
coat
a piece of clothing you wear on top; Joseph's was a special one
jealous
wanting what someone else has, and feeling upset about it
Check yourself

1. Who gave Joseph the coat?

Why: Genesis says Jacob, Joseph's father, gave him the special coat.

2. What does jealous mean?

Why: Jealous means you want what someone else has. The brothers wanted a coat like Joseph's.

3. How many sons did Jacob have?

Why: Jacob had twelve sons. Joseph was one of them.
4.2

Dreams That Bow Down

Main ideaThe story says Joseph dreamed his family would bow to him, and it made his brothers angry.

Joseph had two dreams. In the first, he and his brothers tied up bundles of wheat. The story says his bundle stood up. The other bundles bowed down to it.

In the second dream, the sun, the moon and eleven stars bowed to Joseph. He told his family. His father said, will we all to you?

The brothers got angrier. The story says they hated Joseph even more. Remember these dreams. They come back later in the story.

Read the numbers
What Bowed Down

Genesis 37, King James Version (1611)

Joseph had eleven brothers. What do the eleven stars stand for?

Words to know
bow
to bend low to show that someone is important
Check yourself

1. What bowed down in Joseph's first dream?

Why: Genesis 37 says the brothers' bundles of wheat bowed to Joseph's bundle.

2. How many stars were in the second dream?

Why: The story says eleven stars bowed, one for each brother.

3. How did the brothers feel about the dreams?

Why: The story says the dreams made the brothers hate Joseph even more.
Section 2

Down to Egypt

4.3

Sold by His Brothers

Main ideaThe story says the brothers sold Joseph, and traders took him to Egypt.

One day the brothers were far away with the sheep. Jacob sent Joseph to check on them. The brothers saw him coming. They made a bad plan.

They took his coat. They threw him in a dry hole. Then some came by. The story says the brothers sold Joseph for twenty pieces of silver.

The traders took Joseph to . The brothers dipped the coat in goat blood. They showed it to Jacob. Jacob thought Joseph was dead. He cried and cried.

Think it through
Two Brothers Speak Up

The story says two brothers did not want Joseph hurt. Reuben said, do not kill him. Put him in the hole instead. He planned to come back for Joseph. Judah said, let us not hurt him. Let us sell him instead.

Did Reuben and Judah do enough to help Joseph? What else could they have done?

Words to know
traders
people who travel and buy and sell things
Egypt
a land by the Nile River where Joseph was taken
Check yourself

1. What did the brothers do to Joseph?

Why: Genesis 37 says the brothers sold Joseph to traders for silver.

2. Where did the traders take Joseph?

Why: The story says the traders took Joseph down to Egypt.

3. What did Jacob think when he saw the bloody coat?

Why: The brothers made it look like a wild animal got Joseph. Jacob thought he was dead.
4.4

Pharaoh's Strange Dreams

Main ideaThe story says Joseph explained Pharaoh's dreams: seven fat years, then seven thin years.

In Egypt, Joseph had hard years. He worked as a servant. He even went to jail for something he did not do. But the story says he could explain dreams.

The king of Egypt was called . He had two dreams. Seven fat cows came out of the river. Then seven skinny cows ate them up. Nobody could explain it.

Joseph was brought from jail. He said, the dreams mean the same thing. Seven good years of food are coming. Then seven years with no food. Get ready now.

From the source
Fat Cows, Thin Cows
The story says Pharaoh dreamed he stood by the river. Seven fat cows came up and ate grass. Then seven thin cows came up. The thin cows ate the fat cows. Pharaoh woke up.
Genesis 41, told in simple words from the King James Version (1611) (paraphrased)

Why would thin cows eating fat cows be a scary dream for a king?

Words to know
Pharaoh
the name for the king of Egypt
explain
to tell what something means
Check yourself

1. What is a Pharaoh?

Why: Pharaoh is the name for the king of Egypt in the story.

2. What did the seven fat cows mean, in Joseph's words?

Why: Joseph said the fat cows meant seven good years of food.

3. Where was Joseph before he saw Pharaoh?

Why: The story says Joseph was in jail. Pharaoh sent for him to explain the dreams.
4.5

Saving Up Food

Main ideaThe story says Pharaoh put Joseph in charge, and Joseph stored food for the hungry years.

Pharaoh liked Joseph’s plan. The story says he made Joseph his top helper. Only Pharaoh was more important. Joseph got a ring, fine clothes and a chariot.

For seven years, the crops grew big. Joseph did not let people eat it all. He the extra grain in big buildings in every city.

Then the thin years came. No rain. No crops. People were hungry all over. But Egypt had food. The story says people came from far away to buy it.

Read the numbers
Years of Food, Years of Hunger

Genesis 41, King James Version (1611)

Why was it smart to save grain in the good years?

Words to know
store
to save something and keep it safe for later
grain
seeds like wheat that people grind to make bread
Check yourself

1. What job did Pharaoh give Joseph?

Why: Genesis 41 says Pharaoh put Joseph in charge of all of Egypt.

2. What did Joseph do with the extra grain?

Why: Joseph stored the grain so there would be food in the hungry years.

3. What happened in the thin years?

Why: The story says no crops grew, and people were hungry everywhere.
Section 3

Brothers Together Again

4.6

The Brothers Come to Buy Food

Main ideaThe story says the brothers came to Egypt for food and did not know Joseph.

Back in Canaan, Jacob’s family was hungry too. Jacob sent ten sons to Egypt to buy grain. The youngest, Benjamin, stayed home.

The brothers came to the man in charge. They low. It was Joseph! He knew them right away. But they did not know him. He wore Egyptian clothes now.

Joseph remembered his dreams. The bundles had bowed. Now his brothers bowed. He did not tell them who he was. Not yet.

Think it through
Do You Know Me?

Look at ten tired men with empty sacks. They bow to a man in fine Egyptian clothes. He wears gold. He looks at each face for a long time. One of them has a gray beard now. The man in gold knows every name.

Why do you think Joseph did not tell them who he was right away?

Words to know
bowed
bent down low to show respect
Benjamin
Jacob's youngest son and Joseph's little brother
Check yourself

1. Why did the brothers go to Egypt?

Why: The story says the family was hungry, so Jacob sent the brothers to buy grain.

2. Did the brothers know the man was Joseph?

Why: Genesis 42 says Joseph knew them, but they did not know him.

3. Which brother stayed home?

Why: Jacob kept Benjamin, the youngest, at home the first time.
4.7

Joseph Forgives

Main ideaThe story says Joseph forgave his brothers, and the whole family moved to Egypt.

The brothers came back a second time with Benjamin. Joseph tested them. Judah spoke up. He offered to stay in Egypt instead of Benjamin.

Joseph could not hold it in. He sent his helpers out. He cried out loud. I am Joseph, he said. The brothers were scared. But Joseph said, do not be afraid.

To means to let go of being angry. The story says Joseph forgave. He hugged his brothers. Then the whole family, and old Jacob, moved to Egypt.

From the source
You Meant It for Bad
After Jacob died, the brothers were scared again. The story says Joseph told them, do not be afraid. You meant to do me harm. But God meant it for good, to save many people. I will take care of you.
Genesis 50, told in simple words from the King James Version (1611) (paraphrased)

Joseph says the bad thing turned into a good thing. What good thing?

Words to know
forgive
to let go of being angry at someone who was unkind to you
Check yourself

1. What does forgive mean?

Why: To forgive is to let go of being angry at someone who hurt you.

2. What did Joseph tell his scared brothers?

Why: Genesis says Joseph told them not to be afraid, and he took care of them.

3. Where did the whole family move at the end?

Why: The story says Jacob and all his family moved to Egypt to be near Joseph.
