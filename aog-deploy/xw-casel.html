The four settings · where the competency is practised

CASEL's framework is a set of concentric rings, and a curriculum that only reaches the innermost one is half a framework. Architecture of Grace has something in each.

One thing this framework does not claim

Disclosed here and everywhereCASEL's competencies are a map of what to teach. They are not a measurement scale, and the framework's three domains — Emotional Regulation & Well-Being, Self-Compassion & Growth Mindset, Social Competency & Repair — are not the five competencies renamed. They overlap; they are not the same object; and the instrument that measures the three has not been independently validated against the five, or against anything else.
