Muhammad and the First Words

The Prophet's Story

Muslims believe the words of the Qur'an came to a man named Muhammad. Who was he? Where did he live? This unit tells his story. It tells it the way Muslims tell it.

You will visit a hot desert city called Mecca. You will meet a boy who lost his parents. He cared for sheep. Muslims say an angel came to him in a cave. The angel said, "Read!"

By the end, you will be able to tell the story in order. You will know the words prophet, angel and revelation. You will know why Muslims say "peace be upon him."

The big question

How do Muslims tell the story of Muhammad and the first words of the Qur'an?

When it happened
c. 570

Muslim tradition says Muhammad is born in Mecca.

Age 6

His mother dies; his grandfather Abd al-Muttalib cares for him.

Age 8

His grandfather dies; his uncle Abu Talib raises him.

As a boy

He cares for sheep and travels with the caravans.

Grown up

People in Mecca call him al-Amin, the trustworthy.

c. 595

c. 610

That night

He runs home afraid; Khadijah wraps him in a blanket and comforts him.

After

c. 610–632

After 632

Muslims say his friends gather the words into one book, the Qur'an.

The story

The Camels Come to Town

A boy sits on a rock and watches a long line of camels walk into a city in the sand.

The sun is hot. The ground is dry sand and gray rock. A boy sits on a big stone at the edge of town. He shades his eyes with his hand. Far away, he sees a cloud of dust. The camels are coming.

The camels walk in a long line. Each one carries heavy bags. Inside the bags are cloth, dates, spices and things to sell. Men walk beside the camels. They have come a long way. They are tired and glad to see the town.

The town is called Mecca. In the middle of Mecca stands a building shaped like a big box. People come from far away to see it. The boy has seen it many times. He lives here.

Muslims tell this story about a boy named Muhammad. They say he grew up in Mecca long, long ago. They tell his story with love. In this chapter, you will hear how they tell it.

A City in the Desert

3.1

Mecca and the Big Box

Main ideaMecca is a desert city with a square building called the Kaaba in the middle.

is a city in a hot, dry land. It has sand, rocks and hills. It does not rain much. Long ago, people found a well there. So people came to live near the water.

In the middle of Mecca is a building shaped like a box. It is called the . Muslims believe it is a house built for God. It is very old.

Today Mecca is in a country called Saudi Arabia. Millions of Muslims travel there each year. They walk around the Kaaba together.

3.2

Camels and Caravans

Main ideaMecca was a trading city, and lines of camels called caravans came and went.

Mecca was a busy trading town. People bought and sold cloth, dates and spices. Goods came in on camels. A long line of camels is called a .

Camels can walk a long way without water. That made them good for the desert. The people of Mecca sent caravans out. Some went south in winter. Some went north in summer.

The Qur’an has a short surah about this. It is called Quraysh, surah 106. Quraysh was the name of the family group who lived in Mecca.

The Trustworthy

Main ideaPeople in Mecca called Muhammad "the trustworthy" because he was honest.

When Muhammad grew up, he worked in trade. Muslims say he was always honest. He did not cheat. He kept his word.

People in Mecca gave him a name. They called him al-Amin. That means "the ." A trustworthy person is one you can count on.

Muslim tradition tells a story. The Kaaba was being fixed. Families argued about a special stone. They asked Muhammad to decide. He found a fair way. Everyone was happy.

The First Revelation
Big questionHow do Muslims say the first words of the Qur'an came to Muhammad?
The story

Wrap Me Up

A man runs down a dark mountain and asks his wife to cover him with a blanket.

It is night. The sky over Mecca is full of stars. A man is running down the side of a mountain. His heart is pounding. He is shaking. He does not stop until he gets home.

Inside the house, his wife looks up. Her name is Khadijah. "Wrap me up!" he says. "Cover me!" She brings a blanket and wraps it around him. She sits with him until the shaking stops.

Then he tells her what happened in the cave. Muslims say an angel came to him. The angel said one word: "Read!" Then it gave him words to say. He was afraid. He did not know what it meant.

Khadijah holds his hand. "You are kind. You help the poor. You tell the truth," she says. "God will not let harm come to you." This is how Muslims tell the story of the first words of the Qur'an.

Talk about itKhadijah listened first and then spoke kind words. Why does that help a scared person?

4.1

An Angel Comes

Main ideaMuslims believe an angel named Jibril came to Muhammad in the cave with words from God.

Muhammad was in the cave of Hira. Muslims say he was about 40 years old. It was in the month of Ramadan. He was alone at night.

Muslims believe an came to him. An angel is a being Muslims believe God sends with messages. This angel’s name was . In English, the name is Gabriel.

Muslims believe Jibril brought words from God. He told Muhammad to say them. This night is called the Night of Power. Muslims say it happened about 1,400 years ago.

4.2

The Word "Read"

Main ideaMuslims say the first word the angel said was "Read!", which in Arabic is Iqra.

Muslims say the angel said one word first. The word was "Read!" In Arabic, it is . It can also mean "Say it out loud."

Muslim tradition says Muhammad answered, "I cannot read." Few people in Mecca could read then. The angel said it again. Then the angel gave him words.

Those words are in the Qur’an now. They are the first five lines of surah 96. Muslims believe they were the very first words of the whole book.

Home to Khadijah

4.3

Afraid and Shaking

Main ideaMuhammad was afraid, so he ran home to Khadijah, and she comforted him.

Muslim tradition says Muhammad was very . He ran down the mountain. He went straight home. He was shaking all over.

He asked Khadijah to wrap him in a blanket. She did. She stayed by him. She did not laugh or say he was wrong. She listened.

Being afraid of something new is normal. Muslims say even the Prophet was afraid at first. What helped was a kind person who listened.

Words Over Many Years

Main ideaMuslims believe more words came to Muhammad for about 23 years, a little at a time.

The words did not all come in one night. Muslims believe more came later. Sometimes a few lines came. Sometimes a whole surah came.

This went on for about 23 years. Some words came in Mecca. Later, Muhammad moved to a city called Medina. More words came there.

Muslims call the coming of these words . Muslims believe God sent them. The words came in Arabic. They were spoken before they were written.

4.6

Friends Learn by Heart

Main ideaMuhammad's friends learned the words by heart and wrote them down, and they became the Qur'an.

Muhammad said the words out loud. His friends listened. They said them again and again. They learned them by heart. To is to learn by heart.

Some friends wrote the words down. There was little paper then. Muslim tradition says they wrote on leather, flat bones and stones.

After Muhammad died, Muslims say his friends gathered all the words. They put them in one book. That book is the Qur’an. Muslims still learn it by heart today.

4.7

What a Prophet Is

Main ideaMuslims call Muhammad a prophet, a person who brings a message from God, and add a blessing after his name.

Muslims call Muhammad a . A prophet brings a message from God. Muslims believe there were many prophets. They say Muhammad was the last one.

When Muslims say his name, they add a blessing. They say "peace be upon him." It is a way of showing respect. You may hear a friend say it.

Jewish people and Christians also have prophets in their books. Some of the names are the same. You will meet some of them later in this course.
