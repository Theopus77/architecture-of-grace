Common Core ELA reading literature and informational text (K–5), Common Core literacy in history/social studies (RH, WHST, 6–12), the C3 history and civics indicators (6–8), the Illinois Learning Standards for Social Science 9–12, and the teaching-about-religion principles World Religions uses (CCSS RL/RI/RH / C3 / IL SS 9–12 / Teaching About Religion)

The course reads a scholar's interpretation of ancient texts as literature and as historical sources in a public school, so it cites the reading standards for the band (RL, RI, RH, WHST), C3 history for 6–8, Illinois SS.H 9–12, and one of the six principles in "A Teacher's Guide to Religion in the Public Schools" (First Amendment Center) that the AAR 2010 Guidelines build on; alignment is the course author's judgment, not a certification or an endorsement by any of these bodies.
