GRAND PIANO (audio/piano/grand/)
  Salamander Grand Piano V3 by Alexander Holm (a Yamaha C5).
  https://archive.org/details/SalamanderGrandPianoV3
  SFZ edition: https://github.com/sfzinstruments/SalamanderGrandPiano
  License: Creative Commons Attribution 3.0 Unported (CC BY 3.0)
  https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/
  Changes made here: 30 notes (every minor third, A0 to C8) at velocity
  layers 4, 8 and 12 of 16; the two microphones time-aligned and mixed to
  mono; silence before the hammer trimmed; each note shortened and faded
  out; one shared level for the whole set; encoded as 96 kbps MP3.

UPRIGHT PIANO (audio/piano/upright/) — also used for the honky-tonk sound
  "Upright Piano" sampled by Simon Dalzell of Ivy Audio, from
  VS Chamber Orchestra: Community Edition by Versilian Studios.
  https://github.com/sgossner/VSCO-2-CE
  License: CC0 1.0 Universal (public domain). Versilian Studios grants the
  right to redistribute; credit to Simon Dalzell (Ivy Audio) and Versilian
  Studios is encouraged, and given here with thanks.
  Changes made here: 23 notes (every major third, A0 to C8) at all three
  dynamic layers; time-aligned mono; trimmed, shortened and faded; one shared
  level; encoded as 96 kbps MP3.

The electric pianos and the organs are not recordings. They are built on the
page from simple waves, the same way the drum machine builds its kits.
