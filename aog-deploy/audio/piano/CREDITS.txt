GRAND PIANO (audio/piano/grand/) — also used for the bright piano
  Salamander Grand Piano V3 by Alexander Holm (a Yamaha C5).
  https://archive.org/details/SalamanderGrandPianoV3
  SFZ edition: https://github.com/sfzinstruments/SalamanderGrandPiano
  License: Creative Commons Attribution 3.0 Unported (CC BY 3.0)
  https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/
  Changes made here: 30 notes (every minor third, A0 to C8) at velocity
  layers 4, 8 and 12 of 16; the two microphones time-aligned and mixed to
  mono; silence before the hammer trimmed; each note shortened and faded
  out; one shared level for the whole set; encoded as 96 kbps MP3.
  The bright piano is these same notes with their high end turned up.

UPRIGHT PIANO (audio/piano/upright/) — also used for the honky-tonk and
the soft felt piano
  "Upright Piano" sampled by Simon Dalzell of Ivy Audio, from
  VS Chamber Orchestra: Community Edition by Versilian Studios.
  https://github.com/sgossner/VSCO-2-CE
  License: CC0 1.0 Universal (public domain). Versilian Studios grants the
  right to redistribute; credit to Simon Dalzell (Ivy Audio) and Versilian
  Studios is encouraged, and given here with thanks.
  Changes made here: 23 notes (every major third, A0 to C8) at all three
  dynamic layers; time-aligned mono; trimmed, shortened and faded; one shared
  level; encoded as 96 kbps MP3. The soft felt piano is these same notes,
  softened, as if a strip of felt hung between the hammers and the strings.

HARP (audio/piano/harp/), MARIMBA (audio/piano/marimba/) and
SOFT STRINGS (audio/piano/strings/)
  From VS Chamber Orchestra: Community Edition (VSCO 2 CE) by Versilian
  Studios: recorded by Sam Gossner and Simon Dalzell, samples cut by
  Elan Hickler (Soundemote).
  https://github.com/sgossner/VSCO-2-CE
  http://vis.versilstudios.net/vsco-community.html
  License: CC0 1.0 Universal (public domain).
  https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/
  So nothing here needs permission. We name them because we are grateful.
  - Harp: 21 notes, E1 to F7, about every third note, played mezzo-forte.
  - Marimba: 10 notes, F2 to C7, struck loud with outrigger mallets.
  - Soft strings: 16 notes, C2 to D6, played softly with vibrato: the
    cello section from C2 to A3, the violas from B3 to A4, the violins
    from C5 to D6.
  Changes made here: each note mixed to mono and saved as a 96 kbps MP3 at
  44,100 samples a second, like the pianos; the quiet moment before it
  trimmed; shortened and faded at the end; the rumble below each note and,
  on the softly recorded low harp strings, the hiss above them taken away;
  every note of an instrument brought to one loudness. We measured the
  pitch of every note: the harp sat about 10 cents flat and a few notes
  were further off, so each note was retuned to A = 440. In the library the
  marimba's and the string sections' file names are an octave below the
  notes they sound; the files here are named by the note you hear. On the
  page, a held string note loops smoothly from 2.4 to 5.4 seconds.
  The tape strings (a 1970s tape keyboard, for prog rock) are the soft
  strings, with a hazier tone, a slow wobble, and a tape that runs out
  after eight seconds.

Every other sound on the piano is not a recording: the electric pianos,
the clavinet, the organs (the '70s rock organ too), the accordion, the
celesta, the glockenspiel, the vibraphone, the steel drums, the bells,
the harpsichord, the kalimba, the music box, the choir, the tape flute
and the synths. They are built on the page from simple waves, the same
way the drum machine builds its kits.

El piano · Architecture of Grace · de dónde vienen los sonidos grabados

PIANO DE COLA (audio/piano/grand/), que también es el piano brillante:
  Salamander Grand Piano V3 de Alexander Holm (un Yamaha C5), bajo la
  licencia Creative Commons Atribución 3.0 (CC BY 3.0). Usamos 30 notas en
  tres fuerzas, en mono y en MP3. El piano brillante son las mismas notas
  con los agudos más fuertes.

PIANO VERTICAL (audio/piano/upright/), que también es el honky-tonk y el
piano suave de fieltro: grabado por Simon Dalzell de Ivy Audio para
VS Chamber Orchestra: Community Edition de Versilian Studios, regalado a
todos bajo CC0 1.0 Universal (dominio público). El piano de fieltro son
las mismas notas, suavizadas, como si una tira de fieltro colgara entre
los martillos y las cuerdas.

ARPA, MARIMBA y CUERDAS SUAVES (audio/piano/harp/, marimba/ y strings/):
de VS Chamber Orchestra: Community Edition (VSCO 2 CE) de Versilian
Studios, grabado por Sam Gossner y Simon Dalzell, y cortado por Elan
Hickler (Soundemote). Regalado a todos bajo CC0 1.0 Universal (dominio
público): no hace falta pedir permiso; los nombramos porque estamos
agradecidos.
  - Arpa: 21 notas, de mi 1 a fa 7, mezzo-forte.
  - Marimba: 10 notas, de fa 2 a do 7, fuerte.
  - Cuerdas suaves: 16 notas, de do 2 a re 6, tocadas suave y con
    vibrato: violonchelos abajo, violas en el medio y violines arriba.
Lo que cambiamos: cada nota está en mono y en MP3, como los pianos;
quitamos el silencio del principio; la acortamos y la apagamos poco a poco
al final; quitamos el retumbo por debajo de cada nota y el soplido de las
cuerdas graves del arpa, que se grabaron muy suave; igualamos el volumen
de las notas de cada instrumento. Medimos la afinación de cada nota: el
arpa estaba un poco baja, así que afinamos cada nota a la = 440. En la
página, una nota larga de cuerdas se repite sin cortes entre los 2,4 y los
5,4 segundos.
Las cuerdas de cinta (un teclado de cinta de los 70, para el rock
progresivo) son las cuerdas suaves, con un sonido más brumoso, un vaivén
lento y una cinta que se acaba a los ocho segundos.

Todos los demás sonidos del piano no son grabaciones: se crean en la
página con ondas sencillas, como la caja de ritmos crea sus kits.
