THE SCRIPTURE RECORDS (Be Ye Holy · The Only Way · The Lord's Prayer · El Padrenuestro) — where the voices come from

The words are the King James Version (KJV) of the Bible and, in Spanish, the
Reina-Valera 1909. Both are public domain.

The voices are volunteers reading the KJV for LibriVox (librivox.org), who give
their recordings to everyone as public domain. Readers include Michael Packard (Matthew, the Lord's Prayer), Joyfull (Mateo,
El Padrenuestro), Darren L. Slider
(Leviticus), Jenn Raimundo (Ezekiel) and David Lawrence (1 Thessalonians,
1 Timothy), with the LibriVox volunteers who read Psalms, Isaiah, John, Acts,
Hebrews and 1 Peter. So nothing here needs permission. We name them because we
are grateful.

Each verse was cut from its chapter, set to the same loudness as the others,
and split in two at the reader's pause, so each half is one pad.

The Lord's Prayer is one take, cut at the end of each line.

The other records in this crate (01 to 11) were made for this site with math;
there is nothing in them to credit.
