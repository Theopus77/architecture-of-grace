For the teacher

<b>The order is the pedagogy.</b> Activities 1–6 build the concept of a fraction before any operation on one; 7–11 build statistics from organizing data, not from formulas. Run them in order the first time; after that, any activity works as a warm-up or a re-probe.

<b>Every activity holds three sets</b> — the same skills with different numbers — so the same student can be probed three weeks running without being measured on memory. The teacher panel at the bottom of each page builds the probe row (independent · supported · hints · confidence) for you.

1

<b>Send a link, get a row back.</b> Share any activity's address with a student (or show it on the board). When the page can reach your Sheet, a Send button appears after a finished set — one tap posts one row to the <b>Practice</b> tab of your Google Sheet: activity, set, independent, supported, hints, confidence, and the first name the student types. Nothing else ever travels.

2

<b>A bare link stays private.</b> On a device with no path to your Sheet, the Send button never appears and the page is exactly what it says: nothing leaves the computer. The lock section on every page tells the student the truth for the mode it is in.

<b>Every activity in this series — and every Social Studies activity — opens three doors.</b> A student who wants to see one first taps <b>Show me one first</b> and the page walks a worked example at their pace — watching is instruction, never a trial. The eight problems are the middle door. And after the close card, <b>One more, if you want it</b> offers a single honest stretch — optional every time, and declining is named as a strong move too. The probe row records all three (demo watched · 8-count · stretch), so one page serves your lowest and your highest learner without either being singled out. (The Interior Math and Science Vocabulary pages open the first two doors — the worked example and the set — not yet the stretch.)

<b>Confidence is never a score.</b> The 1–5 at the end of each activity is the student's own read of themselves. It goes in the row so you can watch it move — it is never averaged with anything.
