How do I know that's true?

There is no send button, no upload, no account, no sign-in. Your work is saved on this one computer so you can come back to it — and you can erase it whenever you want.

This page has no way to send anything. It saves your work in this browser's own storage on this machine. It loads nothing from the internet and sends nothing out, so there is no copy anywhere else — not with your teacher, not with the school, not with anyone.

This is practice, not the test. Getting one wrong here costs nothing — it just shows you what to study tonight. The quiz here looks like the real test on purpose, so the real one feels familiar.

Retrying is not cheating. When a wrong tap explains itself and you get it on the second try, that is the studying actually happening. The page keeps count honestly — first-try and second-try are both real, and both are good news.

If you share this computer, erase before you go. Anyone who uses this same browser profile could open the page and see your work. That is what the erase buttons are for, and using them is completely normal.
