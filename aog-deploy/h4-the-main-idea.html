The Main Idea

Every wrong answer here is wrong in a nameable way: too small, too big, or not in the text. Learn the three traps and the main idea starts finding you.

Show me one first

This one is nobody's answer — it is only here to show the moves before you touch yours. Watching it is instruction, never a trial, and it counts toward nothing.

The blacksmith shop opened before dawn. Farmers lined up with broken plows. By noon the coal bin was half empty, and the hammering never stopped until dark.
Try the sizes. Too small: the coal bin was half empty — one sentence, one brick. Too big: blacksmiths were important in history — bigger than this shop.
Just right: the shop was busy from dark to dark. Every sentence works for that one.

Now number 1 is yours. The hint button is there the whole time, and using it is part of the lesson.

Passage 1 of 8 One passage, one idea that covers it.

Need help?

Read each passage. Then write its main idea in your own words — one sentence that every other sentence is working for.

  2. 2At the convention, big states wanted votes in Congress counted by population. Small states wanted every state to count the same. The Great Compromise gave each side half: a House based on population, and a Senate with two seats for every state.Main idea, in your own words:
  3. 3The Constitution splits power three ways: Congress writes the laws, the president carries them out, and the courts judge them. Each branch can block the others. The writers wanted no single branch — and no single person — to hold all the power.Main idea, in your own words:
  4. 4In 1803 France offered to sell the entire Louisiana Territory for fifteen million dollars. Jefferson worried that the Constitution did not clearly allow such a purchase — but the chance was too big to miss. With one signature, the country doubled in size.Main idea, in your own words:
  5. 5Before the canal, moving a ton of flour from the Great Lakes to New York City cost about a hundred dollars and took weeks by wagon. After 1825, the same trip cost under ten dollars by water.
