Smaller year means older

A line with years on it. 1965 is over here, and 1990 is over there.

A SMALLER year means OLDER. Someone born in 1965 is older than someone born in 1990. And on a timeline the oldest goes on the LEFT and the newest on the right — never the other way round.

Two things people get wrong

⚠️ People often think…

The way things were done long ago was worse.

Different is not the same as worse. Playing marbles instead of a video game is just a different game. Some old ways were harder, some were better, and most were simply different.

Different is not worse.

⚠️ People often think…

A bigger year number means a person is older.

It is backwards. A bigger year is more RECENT, so that person is younger. 1990 is a bigger number than 1965, and the person born in 1990 is the younger one.

Bigger year, younger person.

Worth knowing cold

Where the past is kept

The past is not gone. Here is where you can go find it.

  1. Old photos You can see the clothes, the cars, the buildings. Ask who is in the picture and what year it was.
  2. Old tools and things A rotary phone, a typewriter, a butter churn. Each one tells you what a job used to take.
  3. People who remember The best source you have. Ask a grandparent or a neighbor what school was like. They will tell you things no book has.
  4. What stayed the same A teacher, recess, learning to read, friends. Some things are in school then AND now — those matter too.

Helpful Hints

🪜 Then to now — the ladder

“Then” is long ago and “now” is today → we know about then from old photos, old tools, old buildings and the stories older people tell → the tools changed a lot: slate to screen, horse to car, well to faucet, icebox to fridge → the people changed less: kids still learn, families still eat together, neighbors still help → a timeline puts it all in order, oldest on the left.

Clues, changes, and what stayed.

🤝 The whole idea

The past is not gone. It is in old photos, in old tools, and in the people who remember. Ask, and it talks back.

Ask someone who was there.

⚠️ Tricky ones

  • Different is not the same as worse. Marbles instead of a video game is just different.
  • A smaller year means OLDER. Someone born in 1965 is older than someone born in 1990.
  • On a timeline the oldest goes LEFT, the newest RIGHT — never the other way.
  • A teacher, recess, and learning to read are in school then AND now.
  • You have a “then” too — your baby photos are your history.

🎯 How the test will ask

  • Two pictures — “which one is then, and how do you know?” (the clues).
  • An old tool — “what do we use today for the same job?”
  • Three events out of order — “put them on the timeline.”
  • “Name one thing that is the same then and now.”
  • “How can we find out about long ago?” (photos, tools, stories).
