A Book Called the Qur'an

The Book

Many families in Chicago have a special book at home. For Muslim families, that book is the Qur'an. It is kept with care. It is read out loud. Its letters are Arabic. In this unit you will learn what the Qur'an is.

You will meet Amira and Yusuf. You will see how their families hold the book. You will learn what a surah is. You will learn what an ayah is. You will find surahs named for a bee and an elephant.

We will use fair words. We will say "Muslims believe." We will say "the Qur'an says." That way we learn about the book. And we are kind to every friend.

The big question

What is the Qur'an, and how do Muslim families read and care for it?

The story

The Green Book on the Top Shelf

Amira's grandfather reaches for a book that is kept higher than all the other books.

Amira is six. She lives in Chicago with her family. Today her grandfather is visiting. He walks to the tall bookshelf in the living room. He reaches up to the very top shelf. There is a green book there, wrapped in soft cloth.

"Wait," Grandfather says with a smile. "First we wash our hands." They go to the sink together. They wash their hands and dry them well. Then Grandfather takes the book down. He unwraps the cloth with care.

The book has gold letters on the cover. "This is the Qur'an," Grandfather says. "It is our holy book." He opens it from the right side. The letters inside curl and loop. They are Arabic letters.

Grandfather does not read quietly. He reads out loud. His voice goes up and down, almost like a song. Amira sits close and listens. When he is done, he wraps the book again. He puts it back on the top shelf.

A Special Book

1.1

The Holy Book of Muslims

Main ideaThe Qur'an is the holy book of people called Muslims.

A is a book a religion loves. Many religions have one. Muslims are people who follow the religion of Islam. Their holy book is the Qur’an.

Muslims read the Qur’an at home. They read it at the mosque. A mosque is a place where Muslims pray. Many Muslims know parts of it by heart.

You can say the name like this: kur-AHN. The word is Arabic. It means "the reading" or "the reciting." Reciting is saying words out loud.

Main ideaMuslims believe the words of the Qur'an came from God to a man named Muhammad.

Muslims believe the Qur’an came from God. They believe God sent the words to a man. His name was Muhammad. He lived a long time ago in a desert land.

Muslims say Muhammad was a . A prophet is a person who brings a message. Muslims believe his message was the Qur’an. Many Muslims say a blessing after his name.

In this class we say "Muslims believe." People believe different things. We learn what Muslims believe. We are kind about what everyone believes.

How the Book Looks

1.3

Written in Arabic

Main ideaThe Qur'an is written in a language called Arabic.

Open a Qur’an. The letters curl and loop. They are not English letters. They are letters. Arabic is a language spoken in many countries.

Muslims believe the Qur’an came in Arabic. So they read it in Arabic. Many Muslims do not speak Arabic at home. They learn the Arabic words anyway.

The Qur’an has been put into English too. That is called a translation. But Muslims say the Arabic is the real Qur’an.

Think it through
Two Kinds of Letters

Look at the word GOD in English. It has three straight, plain letters. Now picture the Arabic word Allah. It looks like a line with a loop, a tall stroke and a little curve. It means God. Both words point to the same idea, but they look very different on the page.

How are Arabic letters different from the letters you write at school?

1.4

Reading from Right to Left

Main ideaArabic is read from right to left, so a Qur'an opens from the other side.

English goes from left to right. Arabic goes the other way. You read from . Your eyes start on the right side of the page.

So a Qur’an opens from the right. The front cover is where you expect the back. Page one is on the right side. You turn pages toward the left.

This can feel strange at first. Try it with any book. Open it from the back. Now you know how a Qur’an opens.

Caring for the Book

1.5

A High Shelf and Clean Hands

Main ideaMany Muslim families keep the Qur'an high up, wrapped or in a case, and wash before reading.

Many Muslim families keep the Qur’an on a high shelf. Nothing is put on top of it. It is often wrapped in cloth. Some keep it in a or on a stand.

Before reading, many Muslims wash. They wash hands, face, arms and feet. This washing is called . It is a way of getting ready.

These are ways of showing respect. Respect means treating something as important. Not every family does it the same way. But the care is the same.

1.6

A Book That Is Sung

Main ideaThe Qur'an is read out loud, and the reading often sounds like singing.

Most books are read quietly. The Qur’an is different. Muslims read it out loud. This is called reciting. The voice goes up and down.

Some people recite very well. Their voices are slow and clear. It can sound like a song. People sit still and listen.

Children learn to recite too. They start with short parts. They say them again and again. Soon they know them by heart.

1.8

Saying It the Fair Way

Main ideaWe say "Muslims believe" and "the Qur'an says" so everyone is treated fairly.

People believe many different things. In school we learn about all of them. We do not say who is right. We say what each group believes.

So we say "Muslims believe." We say "the Qur’an says." These words are . They tell what the book says. They let each person decide.

Try it now. Say, "Muslims believe the Qur’an came from God." That is a fair sentence. It is true about what Muslims believe.

A Bee and an Elephant

Yusuf finds two chapter names in the Qur'an that sound like a trip to the zoo.

Yusuf is in first grade. His aunt is teaching him to read the Qur'an. She opens the book to a page near the back. "This chapter is called The Elephant," she says. Yusuf laughs. "A chapter about an elephant?"

Then she turns to the middle of the book. "This chapter is called The Bee," she says. "The Qur'an says God taught the bee to build its home." This chapter is longer. Yusuf cannot count all its lines.

A Chapter Is a Surah

Main ideaThe Qur'an has 114 chapters, and each chapter is called a surah.

Many books have chapters. The Qur’an has chapters too. A chapter of the Qur’an is called a . Say it like this: SOO-rah.

The Qur’an has 114 surahs. That is a lot! Some are very long. Some are very short. Each one has a name.

Muslims know many surahs by name. They say the name, not a number. "Let’s read The Opening." "Let’s read The Elephant."

2.2

A Line Is an Ayah

Main ideaEach line of a surah is called an ayah, a word that also means "sign."

Each surah is made of lines. A line of the Qur’an is called an . Say it: AH-yah. More than one are called ayahs.

The word ayah also means "sign." The Qur’an calls the sun a sign. It calls rain and birds signs too. Muslims believe every line is a sign from God.

Ayahs have numbers. So you can find any line. Surah 1, ayah 1 is the first line of the book. We write it like this: 1:1.

Words to know

3. What does 1:1 point to?

2.3

The Opening, Seven Short Lines

Main ideaThe first surah is Al-Fatiha, The Opening, and it has just seven ayahs.

The first surah is called . That means The Opening. It opens the book. It has only seven ayahs.

In simple words, it says: praise God, who is kind. God is in charge of the big day to come. We ask God for help. Show us the good path.

Muslims say Al-Fatiha in every prayer. Many small children learn it first. It is the surah Muslims say most often.

Section 2

Surahs with Animal Names

2.4

The Cow and the Bee

Main ideaSome surahs are named for animals, like The Cow and The Bee.

Surah 2 is called The Cow. In Arabic that is Al-Baqarah. It is the longest surah. A story inside it tells about a cow. That gave it the name.

Surah 16 is called The Bee. In Arabic that is An-Nahl. The Qur’an says God taught the bee. It says the bee builds homes in hills and trees. It says the bee makes a drink that helps people.

A surah’s name comes from one thing inside it. The whole surah is not about that animal. The name is like a label on a drawer.

2.5

The Ant and the Elephant

Main ideaThe Ant tells of an ant who warned her friends, and The Elephant tells of an army that was stopped.

Surah 27 is called The Ant. In Arabic that is An-Naml. It tells about King Sulayman (Solomon). A little ant saw his army coming. She told the other ants to hurry home.

Muslim tradition says this happened long ago. It was around the year Muhammad was born. That was about 1,450 years ago.

Section 3

Finding Your Way

2.6

Long Ones First, Short Ones Last

Main ideaThe long surahs are near the front of the Qur'an and the short ones are near the back.

The Qur’an is not in story order. It goes by size, mostly. The surahs come near the front. The short surahs come near the back.

The Cow is surah 2. It has 286 ayahs. The Elephant is surah 105. It has only 5 ayahs. The very shortest surahs have just 3.

So if you want a short surah, turn to the back. If you want a long one, look near the front. Al-Fatiha is the one exception. It is short, but it comes first.
