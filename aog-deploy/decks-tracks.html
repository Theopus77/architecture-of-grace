No time to gather songs?

The turntables come with a Starter crate: eleven short tracks we made, from 80 to 128 BPM. Press Load the starter crate on the turntables. They work for every lesson.

BPM numbers below are about right. Recordings differ a little. The deck will measure the real tempo when you load a song.

Free and legal

The same free sites the turntables list. Each one says how you may use its music. Check before you share a mix.

ccMixter

Acapellas, beats and remixes shared for remixing.

License: Mostly CC BY or CC BY-NC. Name the artist; BY-NC means no selling.

Looperman

Loops and acapellas from musicians.

License: Loops are free to use. Each acapella has its own terms, set by the person who shared it.

Freesound

Single sounds, voices and samples.

License: Each sound has its own license. CC0 is free; CC BY needs credit.

Pixabay Music

Full songs in many styles.

License: Free under the Pixabay Content License. Credit is not required.

Free Music Archive

Full songs. You can filter by license.

License: Check each track's Creative Commons license. CC0 is free; CC BY needs credit.

Musopen

Classical music recordings.

License: Public-domain recordings. Free to use.

YouTube Audio Library Extra

Free songs inside YouTube Studio (sign in, then Audio Library).

License: Free to use. Some songs ask for credit, and it says which.

How to set up before class

  1. Pick the songs for the lessons you will teach. Use the list above.
  2. Get the files: buy them, or download free ones. Keep them in one folder.
  3. Check each song is the clean version. Listen all the way through once.
  4. On each device, open the Turntables and press Load the starter crate. Then tap Add songs for your own files.
  5. The crate stays on that device only. Do this on every device students use.
  6. Play one song and set a comfortable volume. Test headphones too.
  7. Keep a backup copy of the folder on a USB stick or in your drive.
