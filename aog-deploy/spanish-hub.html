For the teacher

<b>This page is a map.</b> It saves nothing about a student and sends nothing anywhere. Each door opens a unit page; a finished activity sends its one summary row to your Sheet, under the name the student typed, and nothing else ever leaves the device.

<b>Hand out one band.</b> Add the band to the address and the page opens there: <b>/spanish#6-8</b>. Every unit has a short address too — <b>Show on the board</b> prints it large enough for the back row.

<b>Built for a resource room.</b> Short sentences, every term defined where it first appears, three levels on every quiz, and the whole page in Spanish at one tap for a family reading it at home.

This page loads no fonts, scripts, or images from the internet and works with the network off. It keeps nothing about a student and has no way to send anything. It is not a district product and carries no district endorsement.
