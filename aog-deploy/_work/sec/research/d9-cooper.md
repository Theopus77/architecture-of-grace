## Summary

1. Milton William "Bill" Cooper (born 6 May 1943, Long Beach, California; died 5 November 2001, Eagar, Arizona) was a Navy and Air Force veteran who became one of the most-read American conspiracy writers and broadcasters.
2. From 1988 to 1989 he began telling the public, first on computer bulletin boards and then in lectures, that he had seen secret Navy intelligence papers about UFOs and a group called "MJ-12" while serving in the early 1970s.
3. His book *Behold a Pale Horse* (Light Technology Publishing, Arizona, 1991) gathers lectures, reprinted documents and essays. It argues that a secret society, the "Illuminati", steers world events through governments, banks and fraternal orders.
4. From the early 1990s he broadcast a shortwave radio show, *The Hour of the Time*, carried on WWCR (Nashville). He became a leading voice of the 1990s militia and patriot movement.
5. In 1998 a federal grand jury in Arizona indicted him on tax charges. He did not surrender. In 2000 the U.S. Marshals Service treated him as a fugitive.
6. On the night of 5 November 2001 Apache County deputies tried to arrest him at his home on a separate state assault charge. Cooper shot a deputy in the head; the deputy survived. Another deputy shot and killed Cooper.
7. **Part of what Cooper said is documented.** MKUltra, COINTELPRO and Operation Northwoods were real. The Bohemian Grove, the Council on Foreign Relations, the Trilateral Commission and Skull and Bones are real groups with known members.
8. **Much is disputed or unsupported.** The MJ-12 papers, alien treaties, his Kennedy-assassination theory and the "Silent Weapons" document have no provenance that historians or investigators accept.
9. **Some is shown false.** The book reprints the *Protocols of the Elders of Zion*, a proven antisemitic forgery. It tells readers to read "Illuminati" where the text says "Jews". It also repeats "Alternative 3", a 1977 British TV drama made as a hoax.
10. His influence has grown since his death, in prisons, in hip-hop culture and in online movements. Biographer Mark Jacobson (*Pale Horse Rider*, 2018) is the main book-length source on his life.

## Primary sources

All are **KNOWN, NOT RE-CHECKED** unless noted. Band = youngest suitable grade band.

### A. Cooper's own words

**P1. Milton William Cooper, *Behold a Pale Horse*.** Sedona/Flagstaff, AZ: Light Technology Publishing, 1991 (ISBN 0-929385-22-5 as usually cited; UNVERIFIED). Many later printings.
- Where: in print; public and prison libraries; used copies.
- What it shows: Cooper's full argument in his own words. It is a primary source for *conspiracy belief in the 1990s*, not for the events it describes.
- Band: 11-12, and only with teacher framing, because it contains the *Protocols*.

**P2. Cooper, "The Secret Government: The Origin, Identity, and Purpose of MJ-12" (lecture paper, 1989).** Reprinted in P1.
- What it shows: his first public version of the MJ-12 and alien-treaty story, before the book.
- Band: 9-10 (excerpt, alongside the FBI file P10).

**P3. *The Hour of the Time* broadcasts (c. 1992-2001), WWCR shortwave.** Hundreds of episodes survive as fan-archived recordings online.
- What it shows: how he explained current events to listeners, week by week (Waco, Oklahoma City, Y2K, his tax case).
- Caution: fan archives may be edited or mislabeled. Date each tape before quoting it. UNVERIFIED as a set.
- Band: 11-12 (teacher background; short clips only).

### B. Court and police records of his life

**P4. *United States v. Milton William Cooper*, U.S. District Court, District of Arizona, indictment of 1998.** Docket number UNVERIFIED.
- Where: PACER (federal court records; fees apply), or the National Archives regional branch that holds closed Arizona federal cases.
- What it shows: the exact charges, which the popular story often blurs.
- Band: 11-12.

**P5. Apache County Sheriff's Office report and Arizona Department of Public Safety investigation of the 5 November 2001 shooting.** Obtainable by public-records request. UNVERIFIED as to what was released.
- What it shows: the official account of the night. Compare it with accounts from Cooper's supporters.
- Band: 11-12 only (a death and a wounded officer).

### C. The documented government secrets Cooper talked about

**P6. U.S. Senate, Select Committee on Intelligence and Subcommittee on Health and Scientific Research, *Project MKULTRA, the CIA's Program of Research in Behavioral Modification*, Joint Hearing, 95th Congress, 1st Session, 3 August 1977.**
- Where: Government Printing Office print; a PDF has long been posted on the Senate Intelligence Committee website (intelligence.senate.gov; not opened this session).
- What it shows: the CIA director telling the Senate that the agency secretly tested drugs, including LSD, on people who did not know, and that most records were destroyed in 1973.
- Band: 9-10.

**P7. U.S. Senate, Select Committee to Study Governmental Operations with Respect to Intelligence Activities ("Church Committee"), *Final Report*, Books I-VI, April 1976 (S. Rep. No. 94-755).** Book III, *Supplementary Detailed Staff Reports on Intelligence Activities and the Rights of Americans*, covers COINTELPRO.
- Where: GPO; digitized by the Mary Ferrell Foundation and the Senate Intelligence Committee site (not opened this session).
- What it shows: official proof that the FBI spied on and tried to disrupt U.S. citizens, including Martin Luther King Jr. and the Black Panthers.
- Band: 9-10 (6-8 for a simple version: "a government can break its own rules, and citizens can find out").

**P8. Joint Chiefs of Staff, memorandum for the Secretary of Defense, "Justification for U.S. Military Intervention in Cuba (TS)", 13 March 1962 (signed by Gen. L. L. Lemnitzer).** Operation Northwoods.
- Where: National Archives (JFK Assassination Records Collection); National Security Archive posting of 30 April 2001, https://nsarchive2.gwu.edu/news/20010430/ (not opened this session).
- What it shows: top military leaders proposed staged attacks, blamed on Cuba, as a pretext for war. Civilian leaders turned it down.
- Important: this was released in **1997**, six years after *Behold a Pale Horse*. The book could not have cited it. It shows that *some* suspicion of "false flag" plans was reasonable, not that any specific later claim is true.
- Band: 9-10.

**P9. Report of the Commission on CIA Activities within the United States (Rockefeller Commission), June 1975.**
- What it shows: the first official admission of the CIA's domestic spying (Operation CHAOS) and the death of Army scientist Frank Olson (1953) after he was secretly given LSD.
- Band: 9-10.

### D. MJ-12, aliens and Roswell

**P10. FBI file "Majestic 12", FBI Records: The Vault.** https://vault.fbi.gov/Majestic%2012 (not opened this session).
- What it shows: the FBI checked the MJ-12 papers in 1988. The file records that the Air Force Office of Special Investigations told the FBI the document was fake ("completely bogus" is the phrase usually quoted from the file; UNVERIFIED word for word).
- Band: 6-8 (a great "is this document real?" exercise).

**P11. The MJ-12 papers themselves: the "Eisenhower Briefing Document" (dated 18 Nov 1952), the "Truman-Forrestal memo" (dated 24 Sept 1947), and the "Cutler-Twining memo" (dated 14 July 1954, found in 1985 in Record Group 341 at the National Archives).**
- What they show: how a document can be made to look official. Critics (Philip Klass) showed that the Truman signature matches, stroke for stroke, a genuine 1 October 1947 Truman letter to Vannevar Bush, which suggests it was pasted in. The National Archives has issued a statement on why the Cutler-Twining memo is doubtful (no registry number, no file record, and Eisenhower's own records place him elsewhere). Details UNVERIFIED this session.
- Band: 6-8 (images of the pages with the questions to ask).

**P12. U.S. General Accounting Office, *Results of a Search for Records Concerning the 1947 Crash Near Roswell, New Mexico*, GAO/NSIAD-95-187, July 1995.** https://www.gao.gov/products/nsiad-95-187 (not opened this session).
- What it shows: a congressional-requested audit. It found that some Roswell Army Air Field records from 1945-49 had been destroyed without proper authority. It found no record of an alien crash. Both sides quote this report, which makes it excellent teaching material.
- Band: 6-8.

**P13. U.S. Air Force, *The Roswell Report: Fact vs. Fiction in the New Mexico Desert* (1995) and *The Roswell Report: Case Closed* (1997).**
- What it shows: the Air Force's explanation: the debris came from Project Mogul, a then-secret balloon array to detect Soviet nuclear tests. Mogul itself was a real secret, which is part of why the story grew.
- Band: 6-8.

**P14. All-domain Anomaly Resolution Office (AARO), U.S. Department of Defense, *Report on the Historical Record of U.S. Government Involvement with Unidentified Anomalous Phenomena (UAP), Volume 1*, March 2024.**
- What it shows: the most recent official review. It reports no evidence that the government recovered alien technology or made agreements with aliens. It also discusses how stories like MJ-12 spread.
- Band: 6-8.

### E. The groups Cooper named that really exist

**P15. Council on Foreign Relations, *Annual Report* (membership roster printed each year) and the journal *Foreign Affairs* (from 1922).** Founded in New York, 1921.
- What it shows: a real, influential, *public* membership group. Its members are listed.
- Band: 6-8.

**P16. Trilateral Commission membership lists (founded 1973 by David Rockefeller, with Zbigniew Brzezinski as first director).** Jimmy Carter was a member before he became President; Brzezinski became his National Security Adviser.
- What it shows: a real private network of North American, European and Japanese leaders. Cooper was right that it existed and that powerful people joined.
- Band: 9-10.

**P17. G. William Domhoff, *The Bohemian Grove and Other Retreats: A Study in Ruling-Class Cohesiveness* (Harper & Row, 1974).** A sociologist's field study, using the Bohemian Club's own printed rosters.
- What it shows: the Grove (Monte Rio, California; Bohemian Club founded San Francisco, 1872) is real. Powerful men do gather there each July. It opens with a mock-solemn play, the "Cremation of Care". Domhoff argues it builds friendship among elites; he does not find it runs the world.
- Band: 9-10.

**P18. Antony C. Sutton, *America's Secret Establishment: An Introduction to the Order of Skull & Bones* (1983/1986).** Cooper drew on Sutton. Sutton used real Yale membership lists.
- What it shows: a secondary source *inside* the conspiracy tradition, with real lists and a debatable argument. Pair it with Yale's own history of its senior societies (founded 1832).
- Band: 11-12.

### F. The false material in the book, and the proof

**P19. *Protocols of the Elders of Zion*, as reprinted in *Behold a Pale Horse* (1991).** See dossier D8 for the exposure of the forgery: Philip Graves, *The Times* (London), 16-18 August 1921; the Bern trial, 1934-35; the U.S. Senate Judiciary Committee print, *Protocols of the Elders of Zion: A Fabricated "Historic" Document* (1964).
- Band: 11-12 only, and never quoted at length.

**P20. Anglia Television, *Alternative 3*, broadcast 20 June 1977 (ITV network, UK).** Written by David Ambrose; the book version is by Leslie Watkins (1978). UNVERIFIED names.
- What it shows: Cooper's "Alternative 1, 2 and 3" material follows a TV drama. The broadcast has on-screen credits for actors and was meant for 1 April (April Fools' Day); a strike delayed it. Both points KNOWN, NOT RE-CHECKED.
- Band: 6-8 (a fun and safe "how a hoax escapes" lesson).

**P21. "Silent Weapons for Quiet Wars: An Introductory Programming Manual" (reprinted in *Behold a Pale Horse*).** The book says the manual was found in 1986 in an IBM copier bought at a surplus sale. It is presented as a 1979 technical manual.
- What it shows: a document with no known author, no archive, and only a story about how it was found. It is a teaching example for provenance.
- Status: authenticity UNSUPPORTED. The earliest printing and its origin are UNVERIFIED this session.
- Band: 9-10.

### G. Scholarship and biography

**P22. Mark Jacobson, *Pale Horse Rider: William Cooper, the Rise of Conspiracy, and the Fall of Trust in America* (New York: Blue Rider Press, 2018).** The only full biography. Jacobson interviewed Cooper's family, fellow broadcasters and listeners.
- Band: 11-12 (teacher background).

**P23. Michael Barkun, *A Culture of Conspiracy: Apocalyptic Visions in Contemporary America* (Berkeley: University of California Press, 2003; 2nd ed. 2013).** Barkun analyzes Cooper's merging of UFO lore with the older Illuminati and *Protocols* tradition, and his "read Illuminati for Jews" note.
- Band: teacher background.

**P24. Mark Fenster, *Conspiracy Theories: Secrecy and Power in American Culture* (University of Minnesota Press, 1999; rev. 2008).** Treats conspiracy belief as a response to real secrecy and distrust, not as foolishness.
- Band: teacher background.

**Count: 24 primary and key sources (P1-P24).** Of these, 14 are government, court or institutional records (P4-P16, P19's Senate print), 4 are Cooper's own materials or documents he reprinted (P1-P3, P21), and the rest are scholarship.

## Story hooks

1. **Media, Pennsylvania, 8 March 1971.** While the country watches the Ali-Frazier fight, eight citizens break into a small FBI office and carry out every file. One file mentions a strange word: "COINTELPRO". It unlocks the story of the FBI spying on Americans. (Band 6-8.) KNOWN, NOT RE-CHECKED.
2. **A Senate hearing room, 3 August 1977.** The CIA director tells senators that files everyone thought were destroyed have turned up, in the money records. They show the agency gave LSD to people who never knew. (Band 9-10.)
3. **Burbank, California, December 1984.** A TV producer finds a roll of undeveloped film in his mailbox. Printed, it shows a "Top Secret" briefing for President Eisenhower about crashed flying saucers. Is it real? Students get the clues the FBI and historians used. (Band 6-8.)
4. **An English living room, 20 June 1977.** Viewers watch a serious-looking documentary: scientists are vanishing, and there is a secret base on Mars. Phones light up. The ending credits list actors. Fourteen years later, the story is in a best-selling American book as fact. (Band 6-8.)
5. **The Pentagon, 13 March 1962.** The Joint Chiefs send the Secretary of Defense a plan to fake attacks and blame Cuba. The answer is no. For 35 years almost nobody knows the plan existed. (Band 9-10.)
6. **A shortwave microphone in the White Mountains of Arizona, 1990s.** Night after night, a one-legged Navy veteran (Cooper lost a leg in a 1980s accident; details UNVERIFIED) speaks to listeners across the country: "Question everything." Who was listening, and why? (Band 11-12.)

## Illinois & Chicago

Only connections this dossier is confident of:

- **COINTELPRO in Chicago: the Fred Hampton raid (4 Dec 1969).** Hampton and Mark Clark, Illinois Black Panther Party, were killed in a raid by Cook County State's Attorney's police. FBI informant William O'Neal had supplied the apartment's floor plan. The families' civil suit (*Hampton v. Hanrahan*, 7th Cir. 1979) led to a settlement of about $1.85 million in 1982-83 from the city, county and federal governments. DOCUMENTED; amount KNOWN, NOT RE-CHECKED. Band 9-10.
- **The Chicago Council on Foreign Relations (founded 1922; now the Chicago Council on Global Affairs).** A real, public foreign-policy membership group, separate from the New York CFR. It is a local example of the kind of group Cooper called secret; its events and polls are public. Band 6-8.
- **Abraham Bolden, Chicago.** The first Black agent on a presidential Secret Service detail (1961). He said the Chicago office had learned of a plot against Kennedy in Chicago in early November 1963. He was convicted of bribery-related charges in 1964, always said he was framed, and was pardoned by President Biden in April 2022. His account of a Chicago plot is DISPUTED; the pardon is DOCUMENTED. Cooper did not, as far as this dossier knows, write about Bolden. Band 9-10.
- No confirmed Illinois link to Cooper himself was found.

## Hard-to-find finds

1. **The FBI "Majestic 12" file (P10).** Few people who argue about MJ-12 have read the FBI's own file. It shows a federal agency testing a secret-society document the way a historian would, and recording the Air Force's view that it was fake. It is the best single classroom exhibit on this topic.
2. **The 1977 MKUltra hearing transcript (P6).** Much more striking than summaries. It shows how the records survived by accident, in accounting files, after an order to destroy them. It gives Cooper's readers a clear "you were right to worry" moment, grounded in documents.
3. **The GAO Roswell report (P12).** Two pages that both sides quote. It found missing records and no alien crash. It teaches students that "records were destroyed" and "the secret is aliens" are two separate claims.
4. ***Alternative 3* (1977) (P20).** A traceable line from a TV spoof to a best-selling book of "facts". It lets students test a claim without touching the hate material.
5. **The Northwoods memo (P8) set against the book's date.** The memo was released in 1997; the book is from 1991. Laying the two dates side by side shows students how to separate "this later proved true" from "the author had evidence for it at the time".
6. **Barkun's analysis of the "Illuminati for Jews" note (P23).** The most careful scholarly account of how Cooper merged UFO lore with the old Illuminati and *Protocols* stories. It is the fairest way to discuss the hardest part of the book with the owner.

### Teaching notes for the owner's conversation

- Start from common ground: MKUltra, COINTELPRO and Northwoods are real, and Cooper was among the voices telling a mass audience to take government secrecy seriously.
- Then ask one question of every claim: *What is the document, where is it kept, and who can check it?* That question sorts rows 2-10 (a public record exists) from rows 11-19 (no record, or a record with no history).
- Keep the *Protocols* separate, 11-12 only, taught with D8. Say plainly that a writer can raise real questions about power and also pass on a forgery. The lesson is about the text, not the reader.
- Grade bands: K-2 and 3-5 get nothing from this dossier directly. Their "secrets" lessons belong in the codes-and-clubhouses units (safe vs. unsafe secrets). The MJ-12 and *Alternative 3* "is this real?" activities are the youngest suitable use (6-8).
