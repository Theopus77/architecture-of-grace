# Room 207 (Book 5 · The Capstone, Grades 11–12) — editorial review

Read-only review of `aog-deploy/room-207-curriculum.html`, `room-207-lessons.html`, `room-207-cards.html`, `room-207-workbook.html`, `w207-1..5-*.html`, `AoG-Anchor-Charts-207.html` (CHARTS array) and `novels/room-207.json` (full text, 28 chapters + notes). Nothing in the repo was modified.

**Headline.** The 28 lessons, the CHARTS array, the five interactive worksheets and the workbook are consistent with one another. Two things are badly out of sync with the rest: (1) the **Crosswalk Matrix / Marker Guide on the curriculum page** (and the routing tables copied from it into the lessons page) describe a different novel — wrong chapters, wrong characters, invented quotes, and lesson/chart names that do not exist in Book 5; (2) the **scenario-card deck** was written against an older lesson list, so the cards' lesson titles, the facilitation notes inside the lessons, and the actual cards disagree with each other.

Novel facts used below (verified in `novels/room-207.json`): cohort is Marcus, Amara, Sofia, Priya, Darius, Jordan, Maya, Theo, Kezia, Mia (in Pittsburgh for nine years; moved at nine). Hannah has been Priya's best friend "since third grade" / "ten years". Aaliyah is 15. Pepper dies in **October of senior year** (Ch. 5); Darius gets a puppy, Hope, in August (Ch. 26). Ms. Calloway is the counselor who runs the weekly Capstone block; Mr. Yates teaches senior English.
