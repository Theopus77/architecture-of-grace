## Ground truth from the novel (novels/room-18.json)

- **32 chapters** (ONE … THIRTY-TWO) plus "A Note Before You Begin" and "Closing Words". Strand (narrator) chapters: 5, 12, 19, 27.
- **Third grade**, Room 18. Kids are **eight and nine** ("some of them are almost ten"). Amara 9, Priya "nine and three-quarters", Maya 9, Kezia 9, Mia 9, Marcus 8.
- **Mr. Patel** = homeroom teacher. **Ms. Calloway** = SEL specialist for grades 3–5, comes to Room 18 once a week for ~40 min and teaches every SEL lesson. **Mr. Tate** = counselor (in the room for both ★★ lessons). Mrs. Yamashita = aide. Dr. Pam Mendel = Jordan's therapist. Hannah = Priya's best friend (another class). Devon = classmate. Iris = Amara's old friend (moved to Cincinnati). Aaliyah = Marcus's sister (was 3 at the bike incident, is 5 now).
- Marcus's bike lie: he was **six**, Aaliyah **three**, at Grandma's; he tells his parents in **Ch. 9** (first Tuesday of November). He is eight now, carried it two years.
- **Ch. 15 (★★) is Mia's chapter** (the move to Pittsburgh, "I wished us into moving"); Mr. Tate is in the room. **Ch. 20 (★★) is Kezia's chapter** (Forgiveness vs. TRUST — the word "reconciliation" never appears).
- Ch. 23: **seven** forgiveness stories / "seven shapes" (grandfather + chess, two brothers at an airport, bully at a coffee shop, father in prison…). No drunk driver.
- Ch. 25: **Ms. Calloway** introduces grace ("four sizes") and mercy ("strength used kindly"), plus the Grace Audit / Grace Gap / micro-moment. Ch. 27 is a narrator strand chapter (ten boundaries) — no Ms. Calloway guest visit. Ch. 29 = Grace Gap launch; Ch. 30 = Grace with Hard People; Ch. 31 = debrief; Ch. 32 = Charter.
- Toolkit (Ch. 5) = ten tools; the breath is "in 4, hold 1, out 6", not box breathing. No backpack-and-stones metaphor, no Isabella / garden gnome, no "three-strike rule" anywhere in the novel.

- Every ★ / ★★ lesson on the lessons page carries a SAFETY CHECK block; every ★ / ★★ card carries its Safety note; the three ★/★★ worksheets carry 988 / Crisis Text Line. Nothing flagged is missing its protocol.

- Language a counselor would soften: "impoverished models of compassion at home" (U2 L4), "The person drowning in self-punishment is often the LEAST accountable" (student-facing in workbook), "Nadia is a perfectionist", "Brennan is prickly", "autistic brains" (curriculum). None are severe; all have replacements above.
- The curriculum page's ages ("nine and ten", "he is ten now") and the Ch. 15 attribution to Marcus are the errors most likely to embarrass a teacher reading with the novel open.
