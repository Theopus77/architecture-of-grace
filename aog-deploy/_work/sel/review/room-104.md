# Room 104 (Book 4, Grades 9–10) — editorial review

Read-only review of `aog-deploy/room-104-curriculum.html`, `room-104-lessons.html`, `room-104-cards.html`, `room-104-workbook.html`, `w104-1-identity-shield.html`, `w104-2-backpack-audit.html`, `AoG-Anchor-Charts-104.html` (CHARTS array), `room-104-novel.html`, and `novels/room-104.json`. Quotes are exact and greppable.

## The three structural problems (read these first)

1. **Two different Book 4 curricula are wired together.** The curriculum page's crosswalk, marker guide, cohort card and the "Closed-Loop Routing" tables at the top of each unit in the lessons page describe a *novel-based* lesson set (U1·L1 "Identity in a Scattered Container" / "Mask vs. Mirror", U2·L6 "When Self-Forgiveness Is Hard", U2·L2 "The Weight We Carry", U3·L6 "The Agency of Release", U4·L1 "Grace as Gift (Generational)", U4·L7 "Community Charter") with anchor charts named WHO AM I WHEN THE COHORT IS NOT IN THE ROOM, REFUSING THE MASK, CATCHING THE CRITIC SOLO, THE NOTICING KID, CARRYING ALONE IS HEAVIEST, NOTICING IS A GIFT, THE LETTER IN PRACTICE, GRACE ACROSS GENERATIONS, ROOM 104 LEARNED, plus tools (Critic Catch Card, Identity Anchor Card, Letter of Release). **None of these lessons, charts or tools exist** in `room-104-lessons.html` (L1 is "Beyond the Brand", L6 is "The Compassionate Witness", etc.) or in the CHARTS array (tax, battery, anchor, gradient, falsify, signal, perfect, notyet, account, chapters, restore, witness, conflict, forgrec, doors, callin, empathy, gracechal, culture, charity, bearwit, recovery, audit, orchard). A teacher following the crosswalk cannot find the lesson or the chart. Either the crosswalk must be re-pointed at the 28 real lessons/24 real charts, or the missing novel-based lessons must be added.
2. **The scenario-card deck for Units 3–4 is from a different edition than the lessons.** Cards B4-15 through B4-27 in `room-104-cards.html` (and duplicated inside the lessons page) carry lesson titles "THE 24-HOUR RULE", "CALLING IN VS. CALLING OUT" (L2), "THE BOUNDARY OF PEACE" (L3, ★★), "CONTEXTUAL EMPATHY" (L4), "FORGIVENESS AS POWER", "NAVIGATING CANCEL CULTURE", "CHOOSING PEACE", "THE ANONYMOUS ACT", "CHARITABLE INTERPRETATION 2.0", "THE GRACE GAP IN FRIENDSHIPS", "SCHOOL CULTURE ADVOCACY", "MENTORSHIP", "THE CLASS LEGACY", "CULTURE CELEBRATION". Every Unit 3–4 lesson's "SCENARIO CARDS · FACILITATION NOTES" and direct-instruction script cite cards that do not exist (Nia and her mom, Darius and the group project, Amara's friendship rupture, B4-16C Priya's family, Yusuf and his friend, Priya and her study group, Diego's exclusion, the online callout, Marcus and his father, Fatima's teacher, Deja's name, Theo's interpretation, Nia's Grace Challenge, the Forgiveness Audit, Nika's classroom, the team, Theo's teammate, the party invitation, Nia's collapse, Darius's public moment, Nia's team, Darius's neighborhood, Deja's orchard, the teacher's legacy). Units 1–2 cards match their lessons; Units 3–4 need the right 26 cards (or the notes/scripts rewritten to the cards that exist).
3. **The curriculum page misreports what happens in the novel** in 9 of its 17 chapter rows (details in the curriculum table). Several rows describe events that occur to a different character or do not occur at all (e.g. Pepper dies, Amara's grandfather's brush, Sofia and the party, Jordan with Ms. Wong, Priya meets the fourth-grade girl in chemistry).
