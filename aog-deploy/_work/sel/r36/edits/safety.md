# Room 36 safety edits (AOG-R36-V1, agent: safety, 2026-10-23)

Every edit is an exact old → new replacement. Apply them in the order listed (some files have several). A check script
confirmed each old text occurs exactly once in its file, also after the edits before it are applied. The safety page
itself is `room-36-safety.html` (/room-36-safety); these edits point the lessons' "Section 4" references there.

What the rewrites follow: AFSP safe-messaging guidance (speak plainly and calmly; give the way to get help every time;
never treat distress as the sign a lesson worked; no unsourced statistics) and the brief (no clinical labels about children,
nothing a hostile reader could quote, Illinois mandated reporting as the brief states it). Every teacher duty is kept:
who tells the counselor, and when. Where a line said "before U2L6" about something that could be self-harm, it now says today.

Owners: room-36-lessons.html → lessons; room-36-curriculum.html → curriculum; AoG-Anchor-Charts.html → charts;
logic-model.html → the lead (no Phase 2 owner).
