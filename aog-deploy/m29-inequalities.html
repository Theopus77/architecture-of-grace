One answer, or a whole stretch

An equation has exactly one answer. Here it is: x = 5.

Open circle: 5 does not count, x > 5. Filled circle: 5 counts, x ≥ 5. The line under the symbol is the ink in the circle.

Four things people get wrong

⚠️ People often think…

Subtracting a negative flips the symbol.

Adding and subtracting never flip anything. Only multiplying or dividing by a negative does.

Only × or ÷ by a negative flips it.

⚠️ People often think…

A negative answer means you flip the symbol.

The sign of the ANSWER changes nothing. 2x < −8 is x < −4 — negative answer, same symbol, because you divided by positive 2.

Look at what you divided BY, not what came out.

⚠️ People often think…

“At least 8” means fewer than 8.

At least 8 means 8 or more: x ≥ 8. At most 8 means 8 or fewer: x ≤ 8. They sound backward, and they are not.

At least = the floor. At most = the ceiling.

⚠️ People often think…

5 < x shades to the left, because the arrow points left.

Put x on the left first. 5 < x is the same as x > 5, which shades to the RIGHT.

Rewrite it with x first, then draw.

Helpful Hints

🪜 From words to a graph — the ladder

Name the unknown → find the limit number → pick the symbol (is the limit itself allowed?) → solve like an equation → FLIP only if you multiplied or divided by a negative → graph: circle, then arrow → test a point.

Name it, limit it, solve it, draw it, test it.

🤝 The whole topic in one idea

An inequality is an equation whose answer is a whole stretch of the number line instead of one point. Every solving move is the same — with ONE extra rule: a negative multiplier mirrors the number line, so the symbol turns around.

⚠️ Traps the test loves

  • Forgetting the flip when dividing by a negative: −2x < 10 is x > −5.
  • Flipping when you shouldn't: a negative ANSWER does not flip anything. 2x < −8 is x < −4.
  • Subtracting never flips. Only multiplying or dividing by a negative does.
  • “At least” is ≥. “At most” is ≤. They sound backward; they are not.
  • Open or closed? No line under the symbol, no ink in the circle.
  • 5 < x shades to the RIGHT. Rewrite it as x > 5 before you draw.

🎯 How the test will ask

  • A sentence with a limit word — “write the inequality.”
  • A list of numbers — “which are solutions?” (substitute each one and read the sentence).
  • A number line — “which inequality is graphed?” (circle, then direction).
  • A one- or two-step inequality with a negative coefficient — the flip question.
  • A money or goal story — write px + q ≤ r or px + q ≥ r, solve it, and say what the answer means.
  • Someone’s wrong work — find the step where the symbol should have flipped.

✅ Before the test, can you…

  • Turn at least, at most, more than and fewer than into symbols?
  • Decide whether a number is a solution by substituting it?
  • Graph an inequality and read a graph back into symbols?
  • Solve a two-step inequality, flipping only when you should?
  • Explain with 2 < 5 why a negative multiplier flips the symbol?
  • Say what your answer means in the story — and round it sensibly?
