Dear families,

Re: Room 36 begins the week of [Date of the first lesson]

This year, [Teacher name]'s class at [School name] will use Room 36, the middle-school part of Architecture of Grace: a year-long program about handling mistakes, being fair and kind to yourself and others, and making things right. Most weeks, from September to June, the class reads a short piece together, talks it over, and practices one small thing, like naming a feeling or writing a few quiet lines in a notebook of their own.

Three things to know from the start

  • Nothing your child writes privately is collected or graded. Private writing stays private: the teacher does not read it, and it never leaves your child's hands. On class computers it stays on that computer; at most the teacher sees that a sheet is finished (name, sheet, time), never the words. Ordinary schoolwork your child hands in is read as always.
  • Our school uses the Universal Edition, which contains no religious content.
  • Any student may pass on any activity, at any moment. No explaining, no penalty, no follow-up questions.

When the counselor is in the room, and why

Two lessons ask students to think privately about hard things they carry: Rewriting the Script in December, about a belief a student carries about themselves, and The Letter of Release in March, a private letter, never sent, about a hurt they want to set down. In both, the school counselor, [Counselor name], is in the room the whole lesson, so a trained adult is right there if a student needs a quiet word. If a student steps out, the counselor goes with them and the teacher stays with the class.

Three more lessons get extra care: Forgiveness vs. Reconciliation in February (the counselor can come within a minute), and the Grace Audit and the year-end lesson in June.

What we ask of you. The yes-or-no form asks your written permission for those five lessons. Please send it back by [Return date]. If it does not come back, the answer is no, and your child does a calm activity on the same skill instead. The fall notice names four lessons with a little private writing; send it back only if you want your child to do the other activity. Nothing about your child's grades or standing changes either way.

One limit on privacy. If a student tells a school adult that someone is hurting them, that adult must get help. In Illinois, every school employee must call the state hotline when there is reason to believe a child is being abused or neglected.

El crítico usa tres trucos. Catastrofizar (C): «Todo está arruinado». Generalizar (G): «Siempre fallo». Personalizar (P): «Todo es mi culpa». Aquí hay tres pensamientos que tuvo Iván esta semana.
