The engines

Every activity here is one of these kinds. The open ones work now; the three “in the workshop” are still being built for this subject.

Study cards, hints and a practice quiz
Open · every unit page
The vocabulary, the people, the texts and the ideas of each tradition — tap-to-reveal, a hints sheet, a quiz at three levels.
Read the text like a scholar
Open · the Lab tab on a unit page
A passage from the tradition's own scripture, in a public-domain translation, tagged sentence by sentence — who speaks, what is commanded, what is promised, what is taught.
Timeline builder
Open · the Lab tab on a unit page
The tradition's story on one rail — founders, texts, turning points.
Sort: belief, practice, text, place
Open · the Lab tab on a unit page
Cards into bins — and across traditions, which lens says what.
Claim, evidence, reasoning — from the text
Open · the Lab tab on a unit page
State what a tradition holds, cite its own words, explain the link. The writer that makes an essay out of a source.
For the teacher

<b>This page is a map.</b> It saves nothing about a student and sends nothing anywhere. Each door opens an activity page; a finished activity sends its one summary row to your Sheet, under the name the student typed, and nothing else leaves the device.

<b>Hand out one band.</b> Add the band to the address and the page opens there: <b>/religions#6-8</b>. Every door has a short address too — <b>Show on the board</b> prints it large enough for the back row.

<b>In the workshop means not built yet.</b> A unit with that mark has no page behind it and no link, on purpose. When one opens, it appears here with a real door.

This page works offline and loads nothing from the internet. It saves nothing about a student and can’t send anything.
