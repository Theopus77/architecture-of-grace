The Turntables, in pictures

Almost no reading. Tape it to the wall next to the decks. It starts with the Simple bench, then the Full bench.

★Start here · the Simple bench

The turntables open here. Full bench adds the mixing tools; the rest of this guide shows them.

Simple bench · Full benchTap to switch. The page remembers your pick.
On a phone: ShowPick Deck A, B, C or the Mixer. One fills the screen.
Load · Take it off · Start · CUE · Set cueStart plays and stops. Set cue marks a spot. Hold CUE to hear it.
8 padsTap an empty pad to mark a spot. Tap it again to jump there, on the beat. Chop 8 fills all eight.
LoopRepeats 1, 2, 4 or 8 beats, or 4 bars. Tap it again to go on.
SYNC · KEY · SLIP · REV · TAP · ½× · 2×SYNC matches speeds. KEY keeps the tune. SLIP keeps your place. Hold REV to play backwards. TAP the beat to set it. ½× and 2× fix a BPM that is half or double.

1What everything is · Full bench

On an iPad or a computer, the three decks sit side by side. The mixer sits under them.

The song, drawnTall bumps are the drums. The strip under it is a close-up. Tap either one to move.
MASTER · BPMThe yellow number is beats a minute. With SYNC, the other decks follow the MASTER deck.
The recordDrag it to scratch. Hold it still and it goes quiet.
Nudge · 33⅓ · 45Nudge slides the beat a hair. 33⅓ and 45 are record speeds.
Pitch · WIDEWIDE lets Pitch go from half speed to one and a half.
High · Mid · Low · KILL (green)Turn a band down or up. KILL takes it out.
FLAT · Volume · Crossfader sideFLAT puts the EQ and the filter back to the middle.
Filter (blue)Left takes away the highs. Right takes away the lows.
Echo · Reverb · FlangerPick one. Pick the beats. Then raise Amount.
Fader · Vinyl noise · Platter · RecordFader: Blend fades, Cut switches fast. Platter sets how scratching feels (part 3).

3If scratching feels too fast

One button changes it. It sits under the crossfader, on the Full bench. Tap it to change it.

Start hereYour hand moves a lot, the music moves a little. Easiest to control.
When your hand feels steadyThe middle setting.
Like a real recordVery touchy: a tiny move is a big jump in the music. Try it once fine feels easy.

4Four scratches

Green = push forward. Red = pull back. One hand on the record of Deck A, one on the crossfader.

Crossfader stays in the middle the whole time

BabyEasy

Push the record forward, then pull it back. The fader stays still. Say hmm-hmm as you go.

Crossfader open on the push · across on the pull

ForwardEasy

Push forward with the fader open. Slide the fader across, pull back quietly, slide it back. You hear only the push.

Crossfader opens and closes inside one push

ChirpMedium

Open as you push, close before the push ends. A short bird sound.

Crossfader stays in the middle

TearMedium

One push. Then pull back in two pieces: fast, then slow. Fader open.

Before the forward scratch, set Fader: Cut (under the crossfader, Full bench). On Blend you still hear the pull back.

5Lining two songs up

Two songs sound like one when their BPM match. Watch the yellow number on each deck.

NOT LINED UP

Not yet

125 against 120. The beats drift apart.

LINED UP

Lined up

125 against 125. Now move the crossfader.

1 Drag PitchOn the slower deck. Watch its BPM climb.
2 Then NudgeSame speed, but landing late? Nudge it forward a hair.
3 Or press SYNCIt matches the MASTER deck for you. Try by ear first: it trains your ear.

6Swapping to the next song

Slide the crossfader slowly. Count 32 beats from one side to the other.

All the way left: the Left decks onlyAt the start, that is Deck A. The Right decks are silent.
Middle: both sidesWhere a mix happens. Also where you leave it to scratch.
All the way right: the Right decks onlyAt the start, that is Deck B. The swap is done.
Crossfader sideLeft, None or Right. A deck on None plays at any crossfader spot. Deck C starts on None.
KILL LOW, halfway acrossTwo bass drums at once sound muddy. Bring the new song in with KILL LOW on, then swap halfway.

7Your record crate

Under the mixer. It keeps your songs, even after you close the tab.

Made right hereFour records the page makes. Press A, B or C to put one on a deck.
Starter crateEleven recorded songs, 80 to 128 BPM. One press puts them all in Up next.
Up nextAdd songs before you play. Drag a record onto a deck, or press A, B or C.
Previously spunThe last eight records you played. One press puts one back on a deck.
TakesAfter you record: Save as .wav, or put the take on a deck.

Closed the tab by mistake? Open the turntables again. Each record goes back on its deck, stopped where you left it.

8Nothing is happening

  • No sound from Deck AThe crossfader is all the way right. Slide it back to the middle.
  • No sound at allPress Start. It is the red one.
  • The record will not movePut a song on first. An empty deck has nothing to turn.
  • Way too fast to controlIt is on 1:1 vinyl. Tap it until it reads Platter: fine.
  • The file will not loadA song from a streaming app is locked. Use a file you own.
  • My song is goneLook in Previously spun and press A, B or C.
  • Two songs sound muddyTwo bass drums at once. Press KILL LOW on one.
