For the teacher

The course follows the arc of a standard junior-high U.S. history textbook — units, chapters, sections, numbered lessons — in original text written at a grade 6–8 reading level. Every lesson has a main idea, a three- or four-paragraph reading with key words you can tap, a source, a data table or a scenario to think through, and three checks.

Trusted sources to read alongside the course

  • Common Core State Standards for Mathematics (corestandards.org): what each grade is expected to learn.
  • Illustrative Mathematics: a free, respected K–12 curriculum with problems and teacher notes.
  • NCTM, Principles to Actions (2014): how strong math teaching works.
  • Van de Walle, Elementary and Middle School Mathematics: Teaching Developmentally: the classic methods book.
  • Jo Boaler, Mathematical Mindsets: mistakes, visuals and a growth mindset in math.
