For the teacher

The course reads the Tanakh — Torah, Prophets and Writings — as Jewish tradition has read it and as historians and literary readers read it, in a public school: its stories for the youngest readers, the shape of the book and its big story in the middle grades, the parts and the ways of reading in 6–8, and close reading with the commentators in high school. Beliefs are attributed (“Jewish tradition teaches…”), the text is quoted from the public-domain JPS 1917 translation or paraphrased plainly, and every lesson ends with three checks answerable from its reading.
