For the teacher

The course teaches the history of sports as institutions: kinds of play, fields and fair turns for the youngest readers; how baseball, basketball, football, soccer, track, swimming and tennis got their lines and rules in 3–5; who was written out, the Olympics, Title IX, disability sport and Illinois rooms in 6–8; college sport, media, records, protest, leagues and eligibility in high school. It is history, not PE: no play, drill or technique is taught. Every lesson ends with three checks answerable from its reading.
