The Ancient Eastern Cosmos

How the peoples of the ancient Near East pictured the world: a flat earth under a solid vault, with waters above and below. This is that picture as it appears in the surviving texts and art, not a modern scientific claim.

Sources

The picture here is drawn from the literature and iconography of Mesopotamia, Egypt, and the Hebrew Bible, roughly the second and first millennia BCE. It is not a single official doctrine. It is a shared set of images: a flat inhabited earth, a solid vault, waters above the vault, waters below the earth, and pillars or mountains holding it up.

Primary witnesses include the Enuma Elish, the Egyptian sky-goddess Nut arched over Geb, and the biblical firmament in Genesis 1.

The layers

Waters above

Above the vault is a sea. Rain is that sea let through windows or sluices in the vault. Genesis 7:11 speaks of the windows of heaven opening.

The vault

The firmament (Hebrew raqia, a beaten-out plate) is solid. The sun, moon, and stars are set in it or travel under it. Nut in Egypt is the sky as a body arched over the earth.

The earth

The inhabited world is a disk or a rectangle, with mountains at the edges. It does not move.

Waters below

Under the earth is the deep. Springs and the sea come from it. In some texts the dead are there too.

A geocentric view

Geocentric here means the inhabited earth is the fixed center and the lights move around it. That is the picture these texts draw. It is not the later Greek mathematical model of Ptolemy, and it is not a claim about modern astronomy.

See Geocentric and Heliocentric for the later mathematical models of the same sky.

Key texts

Enuma Elish

After defeating Tiamat, Marduk splits her body. Half becomes the sky. He sets the stations of the gods as the stars.

Genesis 1

God makes the firmament to separate the waters above from the waters below, then sets the lights in the firmament.

Egypt

Nut, the sky, is held up over Geb, the earth. Shu, the air, stands between them. The sun travels through Nut by day and through the underworld by night.

Later models

Greek natural philosophers from the sixth century BCE onward proposed a spherical earth and, later, a spherical cosmos of nested shells. That is a different picture from the vault. The Antikythera mechanism is a geared model of that later, spherical sky.
