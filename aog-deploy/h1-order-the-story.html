Social Studies · Activity 1

Order the Story

History is not a pile of dates. It is one thing making the next thing possible. Put the cards in order and the story tells itself.

Show me one first

This one is nobody's answer — it is only here to show the moves before you touch yours. Watching it is instruction, never a trial, and it counts toward nothing.

Gold is found · Thousands rush west · News spreads east · California asks to join the Union
Find the beginning: nothing on this list can happen until the gold is found. That card goes first.
Now ask of each card: what did the last one make possible? Found gold → news spreads → people rush → a crowded place asks to be a state.
Gold (1848) → news (1848) → the rush (1849) → statehood asked (1849). The order was inside the story all along.

Now number 1 is yours. The hint button is there the whole time, and using it is part of the lesson.
