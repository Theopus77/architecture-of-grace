What this page is not

This is an alignment document, not an efficacy claim. It shows which standard each lesson is written against. It does not show that the lesson produces an outcome, and no efficacy study of this curriculum has been conducted — see Evidence & Roadmap, which says so at more length and commits to not claiming otherwise.

Alignment here means the benchmark was selected by the author while writing the lesson and printed with it. It has not been reviewed by ISBE, by CASEL, or by any third party, and neither organization has endorsed this curriculum.

The same holds for every part added here. The course and bench codes were chosen by the course author from each unit’s chapters. No state board, standards body or publisher has reviewed them, and none has endorsed this curriculum.

Where we could not be sure of an exact code, we cite the family and say so, rather than guess.

What could not be mapped honestly

  • Daily Drafts are not tagged item by item. Each subject points to the standards of its course for the same grade band. We do not invent a code for each question.
  • Practice rooms carry the standards of their units. A room is linked to the unit pages that send students there; it has no codes of its own.
  • The benches have no grade band. Each lesson group cites the band where its task sits. Music is cited at the process level (for example MU:Cr2.1), without a grade letter.
  • Illinois health is cited by goal only (Goal 22, Goal 23). The National Health Education Standards carry the unit-level codes.
  • The faith-text courses are academic study. They cite reading and history standards and the teaching-about-religion principles. No standard asks a student to believe or practice anything.
  • Adult editions are out of scope. K–12 standards do not cover adult learners.
Found something that overstates? This site commits to not using “evidence-based”, “proven” or “validated” before the evidence exists. If a line on this page gets ahead of what is behind it, that is a defect — say so and it will be corrected.

SEL lessons, lesson by lesson

A lesson-level crosswalk covering 140 lessons across five grade bands. It is assembled from the curriculum itself — the alignment block printed inside each lesson, and the K–12 crosswalk generated from the five curriculum volumes. Nothing on this page was written for the page.

136 of 140 lessons carry an Illinois SEL benchmark, drawing on 75 distinct benchmarks across all three Illinois goals. 119 of 140 name a primary CASEL competency. 67 are additionally mapped to the companion novel — the chapter the lesson sits against, and the anchor chart built from it.

On this page in Spanish. The framing, the headings and every explanation switch with the language button. The table cells do not. Lesson titles are the titles as printed in the books, and the Illinois benchmark text is the wording the Illinois State Board of Education publishes — in English. An unofficial translation of a state standard would misrepresent it, and a lesson title that appears in no book would send a teacher looking for something that does not exist. Both stay in the original on purpose.
