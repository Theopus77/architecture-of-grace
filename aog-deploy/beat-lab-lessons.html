Lesson 1 · Skill

Meet the pads

Sixteen pads. How you tap them changes the sound.

  1. Tap any pad
  2. Tap a pad right in the middle: a loud hit
  3. Tap a pad near its edge: a soft hit
  4. On bank A, play pads 1 to 8

Try this: Play a beat with your two pointer fingers: kick on pad 1, snare on pad 2.

Think about it

Which pad sounds lowest? Which sounds highest?

Your turn

Make up a pattern with three pads. Write their numbers in order.

Lesson 2 · Skill

Start a beat

There are 105 beats ready. Pick one and press Play.

  1. Pick a beat in Start from a beat
  2. Press ▶ Play
  3. Press ■ Stop

Try this: Pick a beat from a country you have never been to.

Think about it

What did the line under the bank say about your beat?

Your turn

Name your three favourite beats.

Lesson 3 · Skill

Fast and slow

Tempo is how many beats a minute. It sets the mood.

  1. Change the tempo
  2. Make it fast: 120 or more
  3. Make it slow: 80 or less

Try this: Play the same beat at 70, then at 130. Does it feel like a different song?

Think about it

What tempo feels like walking? Like running?

Your turn

Tap your foot to a song you like. Guess its tempo.

Lesson 4 · Skill

The steps

Each pad has steps under the pads. A lit step is a hit.

  1. Tap an empty step to add a hit
  2. Tap a lit step to take a hit away
  3. Press ▶ Play

Try this: Pick the kick. Add a hit on step 11. Hear the bounce.

Think about it

How many steps are in one bar?

Your turn

Draw 16 boxes. Fill the ones where your kick plays.

Lesson 5 · Skill

Record a beat

Record the loop keeps what you tap. You hear it the next time round.

  1. Turn on ● Record the loop
  2. Tap four hits while it records
  3. Press ↶ Undo
  4. Press Clear the loop

Try this: Record the hi-hat on every beat, then the snare on 2 and 4.

Think about it

Undo took back your last change. When would that help?

Your turn

Record a beat with only two pads. Make it groove.

Lesson 6 · Skill

Line up and swing

Line up moves your taps onto the beat. Swing makes them bounce.

  1. Set Line up taps to: 16th notes
  2. Set Line up taps to: Exactly as played
  3. Set Swing to 54 % or more
  4. Press Play with swing on

Try this: Record hi-hats with Swing at 50, then at 62. Which one makes you nod?

Think about it

Why might a drummer play a little late on purpose?

Your turn

Write a tempo and a swing that feel good together.

Lesson 7 · Skill

Rolls with Repeat

Repeat plays a held pad again and again, in time.

  1. Turn on Repeat
  2. Hold a pad: it rolls by itself
  3. Change the Repeat speed
  4. Record a roll while the loop plays

Try this: Hold the hi-hat at 1/16, then 1/32 for one beat. That is a trap roll.

Think about it

Where in a song would a roll fit best?

Your turn

Record a roll that leads into beat 1.

Lesson 8 · Skill

Pick your drums

Bank A has two kits: one on pads 1 to 8, one on 9 to 16.

  1. Change the kit for pads 1 to 8
  2. Change the kit for pads 9 to 16
  3. Play a pad from 9 to 16
  4. Turn on Every hit full

Try this: Put a rock kit on 1 to 8 and Latin percussion on 9 to 16. Mix them.

Think about it

Which kit would you pick for a calm song? Why?

Your turn

Name the two kits you like best together.

Lesson 9 · Skill

Chords

Bank B plays chords. Pads 1 to 8 are the key's chords.

  1. Pick bank B · Chords
  2. Tap pads 1, 5, 6, 4, in that order
  3. Change the key on bank B
  4. Play a seventh chord: pads 9 to 15

Try this: Tap 1, 5, 6, 4 slowly, then fast. Same chords, two feelings.

Think about it

Which chord sounds like home?

Your turn

Write a four-chord pattern with the pad numbers.

Lesson 10 · Skill

Happy and sad chords

Major sounds bright. Minor sounds moody.

  1. Pick bank B · Chords
  2. Set bank B's scale to Minor
  3. Change bank B's instrument
  4. Tap pads 1, 5, 6, 4, in that order

Try this: Play 1, 5, 6, 4 in Major, then in Minor. Tell someone which one is sad.

Think about it

Name a song that sounds Minor to you.

Your turn

Pick an instrument and a mood for a song you could make.

Lesson 11 · Skill

Notes and melodies

Bank C plays one note per pad, low to high, in a scale.

  1. Pick bank C · Notes
  2. Play pads 1 to 8 going up, one by one
  3. Change bank C's scale
  4. Change bank C's instrument
  5. Change bank C's range

Try this: In Minor pentatonic, every pad sounds good with every other. Play freely.

Think about it

Why do a bass and a flute sound so different on the same pad?

Your turn

Write a five-note melody with pad numbers.

Lesson 12 · Skill

Chop a record

Bank D cuts a record into sixteen beats. Each pad plays one.

  1. Pick bank D · Chops
  2. Play four different chops
  3. Pick another record to chop
  4. Start the chops at another bar
  5. Press Use its tempo

Try this: Find the chop with a drum hit. Play it on beat 1 of every bar.

Think about it

How is chopping a record like making a collage?

Your turn

Write the numbers of the three chops you like best.

Lesson 13 · Skill

Mix the pads

Each pad has its own volume, pitch, and place left or right.

  1. Change a pad's Volume
  2. Change a pad's Pitch
  3. Move a pad left or right
  4. Press Reset this pad

Try this: Turn the hi-hat down to 70 %. Hear the kick and snare come forward.

Think about it

Why not make every pad as loud as it can be?

Your turn

Which pad would you move left, and which right?

Lesson 14 · Skill

Keep your loop

Make the loop into one file, with no gap. Save it or send it on.

  1. Press Make the loop into a file
  2. Press Save as .wav
  3. Press Send to the Mixing Desk
  4. Press Send to the turntables

Try this: On the turntables, play your loop and scratch over it.

Think about it

What is the difference between the loop and a take?

Your turn

Give your loop a name.

Lesson 15 · Skill

Play live, and record it

A take keeps everything you play, like a recording of a show.

  1. Turn on Click
  2. Press ● Record a take to keep
  3. Play, then stop recording

Try this: Let the loop play and add drums live on top for 30 seconds.

Think about it

What changed between your first take and your second?

Your turn

Plan a 30-second show: what plays first, what comes in later?

Lesson 16 · A song to make

Song: Boom bap

A 90s hip-hop track: drums, chords and bass.

  1. Pick Boom bap (90s hip-hop)
  2. Set Loop length to 4 bars
  3. Record four chords on bank B
  4. Record four bass notes on bank C
  5. Press Make the loop into a file

Try this: Bass on the same beats as the kick makes it hit hard.

Think about it

Which part did you make first? Why?

Your turn

Write the four chords and four bass notes you used.

Lesson 17 · A song to make

Song: Lo-fi study

A calm, swinging loop to study to: soft drums, minor chords, a chop.

  1. Pick Lo-fi study beat
  2. Set Swing to 54 % or more
  3. Bank B in Minor, with chords recorded
  4. Record a chop on bank D
  5. Press Send to the Mixing Desk

Try this: Turn the drums down and pitch the chop down 3 steps for a warm, dusty sound.

Think about it

What makes music good to study to?

Your turn

Name your lo-fi track and describe it in five words.
