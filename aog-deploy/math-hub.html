The engines

Every activity here is one of these kinds, and all six work now.

Move it with your hands
Open · the m- and c-pages
A number line you walk, a balance you load on both pans, an area rectangle read both ways, towers made even. Every page opens three ways: watch one first, work the set, or take one more.
Test prep that names the mistake

Ten daily lines, with room to work

Daily Practice: column algorithms worked from the ones place, long division as a staircase, fractions read across the line, and the error named when it is certain.
Label, graph, sort, order, argue
Open · the Lab tab
The Lab every science and social studies unit carries — table to graph, put the steps in order, claim-evidence-reasoning — ready for the math rooms as they are built.
The coordinate plane
Open · Functions and slope
Tap a point card, then tap the grid: plot a table, watch the line draw itself, read its slope and where it starts. Two lines that draw themselves to their crossing, a triangle flipped corner by corner, and the fourth corner of a garden found on a first-quadrant grid run on it too.
The shape bench
Open · the geometry rooms
Tap two opposite corners and a rectangle is laid on the figure. Cut a garden, or a building plot, into rectangles you choose — every cut that covers it with no gap and no overlap is accepted, because that is what cutting a shape into rectangles means. Then read the drawing back: the lengths nobody printed, the area, the fence around it, and what the area becomes when every length is doubled. On the angles room it is a protractor instead of a grid, with two scales printed the way a real one has them: the reading counts as right when it is close, and every angle deduced from it has to be exact.
