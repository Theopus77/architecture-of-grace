  2. 2
    Self-CompassionKnow the signs of burnout. Check and refill the team's tank.
  3. 3
    ForgivenessThe Repair Round. Making redos normal in the building.
  4. 4
    GraceTrust norms, leading through grace, and keeping the culture going.

Staff talk: "Benefit of the Doubt"

When a staff frustration comes up (a missed deadline, a tense email), the team pauses. Together, name three kind reasons the person might have acted that way, then respond. It moves a building from suspicion to trust.

For leaders: model it first

Staff culture is caught, not mandated. For one month, lead with the four pillars yourself: name a teacher's character out loud, say when your own tank is low, repair a misstep with staff, and open every meeting with connection before the agenda.
