Mathematics · Activity 1

One-Step Equations

An equation is a balance. Whatever you do to one side, you do to the other — and you keep going until x is standing alone.

Watching one first is a strong way to start.

Problem 1 of 10 Get x alone.

Do the same thing to both sides

Pick a move. It happens to the left side and the right side at once — that is what keeps the balance true.

Need help?
