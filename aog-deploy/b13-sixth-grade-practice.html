Helpful Hints

📐 Decimals: three moves

Add/subtract: LINE UP the points, fill gaps with zeros. Multiply: multiply, then count the decimal places in the problem. Divide: move the point in BOTH numbers until you divide by a whole number — 0.0648 ÷ 0.12 becomes 6.48 ÷ 12.

Line up · count places · move together.

🧮 GCF small, LCM big

GCF: the biggest number that divides IN to both (GCF of 14 and 77 is 7). LCM: the first skip-count number both share (LCM of 12 and 15 is 60). The LCM can even BE one of the numbers: LCM of 4 and 8 is 8.

If your GCF is bigger than the numbers, you found a multiple.

🍕 The four fraction rules

Same bottoms: add/subtract the tops. Different bottoms: LCM first, then equivalent fractions. Multiply: straight across. Divide: Keep–Change–Flip. Mixed numbers: improper fractions FIRST, and simplify at the end — always.

The bottoms never add. The second fraction is the only one that flips.

🔔 Rounding and PEMDAS, quick

Rounding: underline the place, look at the digit after it — 5 or more rounds up. Order of operations: parentheses, exponents (2³ = 8), then × and ÷ left to right, then + and − left to right.

2³ = 2 × 2 × 2 = 8 — never 6.

🗺️ The plane in one breath

(x, y): x runs right/left first, y jumps up/down second. Quadrants go counterclockwise from top right: I, II, III, IV. A zero in the pair parks the point ON an axis — no quadrant.

Run, then jump. Zero stands in the doorway.
