iWhat everything is

The eyepiece is on the left. Every control is in the panel on the right. The knobs are under the eyepiece.

Lessons (teacher)Locked: each lesson opens the next control. Open: every control is out. Lock it before a student sits down.

LampNo light, no picture.
Stage clipsSeated holds the slide still.
ObjectiveHow big. 4× is the smallest and the safest.
LensesQuartz is the purest glass: no colour fringe, a crisper edge. Glass shows a violet fringe. Light is still the limit.

FineSmall focus moves. The only knob on 40× and 100×.

EyepieceTurns the eyepiece. Finds dust on the glass.
Working distanceHow far the lens is from the glass. Red means too close.
StainColours parts of a cell. The choices change with the slide.

?Something looks wrong

  • The circle is blackLamp: On. Then check the lamp before you blame the focus.
  • It is blurry4× or 10×: Coarse. 40× or 100×: Fine only.
  • Cracked glassCoarse on high power did it. Press New slide, start again on 4×.
  • Too bright and washed outTurn the Diaphragm down.
  • Too dark around the edgeTurn the Diaphragm up.
  • A speck that will not move when you dragTurn the Eyepiece knob. If the speck turns with it (compare the two pictures), it is dust on the eyepiece, not on the slide.
  • Dim and soft on 100×Put the oil on.
  • The slide keeps slidingStage clips: Seated.
