Our song, in order (60 bars)

  1. Intro: the line once (bars 1–4)
  2. Verse: the line 4 times (bars 5–20)
  3. Chorus (bars 21–28)
  4. Verse: the line 4 times (bars 29–44)
  5. Chorus (bars 45–52)
  6. Chorus again, and end on D (bars 53–60)

The record also has a guitar solo and one more verse. You can add them later.

Print this page and keep the map next to you while you play.

1Set the song

Bad Moon Rising is fast (180 beats a minute) and its home chord is D.

2The drums: boom-chick

Drum Machine · the whole song

Kick on beats 1 and 3, snare on 2 and 4, a tick on every beat. Measured from the record.

Bad Moon Rising was written by John Fogerty and recorded by Creedence Clearwater Revival in 1969. This lesson teaches how to play its music: the chords, the beat and the bass. No words from the song are here.
