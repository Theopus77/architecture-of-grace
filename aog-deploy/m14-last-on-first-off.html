Mathematics · Activity 14

Last On, First Off

Now x is wrapped twice — multiplied, then added to. Unwrap it like shoes and socks: whatever went on last comes off first, and whatever you do to one side, you do to the other.

Show me one first

This one is nobody's problem — it is only here to show the moves before you touch yours. Watching it costs nothing and is written down nowhere.

2x + 5 = 9
Two wraps: x was multiplied by 2, then 5 was added.
Last on, first off: − 5 from both sides → 2x = 4.
÷ 2 on both sides → x = 2.

Now problem 1 is yours. The hint button is there the whole time, and using it is part of the lesson.

Watching one first is a strong way to start.

Problem 1 of 10 Get x alone — two undos this time.

Peel the wraps off x

Tap a wrap on the balance to peel it off. Whatever a peel does to the left side happens to the right side at the same moment — that is what keeps the balance true.
