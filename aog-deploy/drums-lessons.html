Tempo is how fast the beat goes. It is counted in beats per minute.

On the machine

  2. Slide Tempo down to 80 and play it.
  3. Slide Tempo up to 120 and play it.

Lesson 3 of 17

The grid: 4 beats

Sixteen boxes make one bar. Every fourth box is a beat: 1, 5, 9, 13.

On the machine
  1. Clear the beat.
  2. Set Length to 16.
  3. Tap the Kick row on boxes 1, 5, 9 and 13.
  4. Play it and count 1, 2, 3, 4 out loud.

Lesson 7 of 17

Swing

Swing pushes the in-between hits late. The beat leans.

On the machine
  1. Use your eighth-note hat beat from Lesson 5.
  2. Press Swing until it says 58%. Play it.
  3. Press Swing until it says 67%. Play it.
  4. Go back to 50% and listen again.

Lesson 14 of 17

Mute, solo and arrangement

Mute takes a drum out. Solo leaves only one in. Then bring everything back.

On the machine
  1. Play a full beat with kick, snare and hats.
  2. Press MUTE, then tap the kick pad. Listen.
  3. Press SOLO, then tap the hat pad. Listen.
  4. Turn every mute and solo off.

Write your own 8 bars

Four parts with beats in them, chained into a track that is eight bars long.

On the machine
  1. Build beats in Parts 1, 2, 3 and 4. Part 4 can be a fill.
  2. Clear the track (CLR).
  3. Add parts until the track is eight long. Repeats are fine.
  4. Play the track from the top.

Lesson 16 of 17

Perform it live

While the beat runs, take drums out and put them back with your hands.

On the machine
  1. Play your eight-bar track.
  2. While it plays, press MUTE and take the kick out for a bar. Put it back.
  3. Press SOLO and leave only the hats for a bar. Then bring everything back.
  4. Stop on beat 1.
