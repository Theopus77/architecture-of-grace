On the machine

  2. Slide Tempo down to 80 and play it.
  3. Slide Tempo up to 120 and play it.

The grid: 4 beats

Sixteen boxes make one bar. Every fourth box is a beat: 1, 5, 9, 13.

On the machine
  1. Clear the beat.
  2. Set Length to 16.
  3. Tap the Kick row on boxes 1, 5, 9 and 13.
  4. Play it and count 1, 2, 3, 4 out loud.

Hi-hats: eighths, then sixteenths

A hat on every other box keeps the time. A hat on every box drives it. An open hat lets it breathe.

On the machine
  1. Start from your backbeat.
  2. Closed hat on boxes 1, 3, 5, 7, 9, 11, 13 and 15. Count 1 and 2 and 3 and 4 and.
  3. Now fill in the gaps: a closed hat on all 16 boxes.
  4. Put an open hat on box 15 and play it.

  3. Change part 2: move a kick, add a fill.

Lesson 9 of 17

Mute and solo, live

Mute takes a drum out. Solo leaves only one in. Do it with your hands while the beat runs.

On the machine
  1. Play your beat.
  2. Press MUTE, then tap the kick pad. It drops out. Tap it again to bring it back.
  3. Press SOLO and tap the hat pad. Only the hats play for a bar.
  4. Bring every drum back while it plays. Stop on beat 1.

Lesson 10 of 17

Boom-bap swing

The 1990s sampler sound. A slow tempo, deep swing, and a snare that leans back a little.

On the machine
  1. Set the tempo between 84 and 96. Set SWING to 58% or 63%.
  2. Kick on box 1 and one kick off the beat, like box 11. Snare on 5 and 13.
  3. Full bench: set TC to OFF. Press REC and PLAY, and tap a snare just after the beat.
  4. Play it back. Nod your head. That lean is the boom-bap feel.

Lesson 11 of 17

Ghost notes

Quiet snares between the loud ones make a beat sound played by a person.

On the machine
  1. Tap the snare on 5 and 13 twice each, so they are accents.
  2. Add two or more soft snares (tap three times) in other boxes, like 8, 10 or 16. Those are ghost notes.
  3. Give the closed hats a shape: one accent and one soft, or more.
  4. Play it. Listen for the ghosts under the backbeat.

Lesson 12 of 17

Trap hi-hats and rolls

A fast tempo, a half-time snare, and hat rolls. TC 1/32 lets a hat land between two boxes.

On the machine
  1. Set the tempo between 130 and 150. Put the snare or clap on box 9 only. That is half time.
  2. Closed hats on every other box, then four in a row somewhere for a roll.
  3. Full bench: set TC to 1/32. Press REC and PLAY, and tap a hat between two boxes.
  4. Play the trap beat. The hat between the boxes is a 32nd note.

Four-on-the-floor house

A kick on every beat, a clap on 2 and 4, and an open hat on every off-beat.

On the machine
  1. Set the tempo between 120 and 128.
  2. Kick on 1, 5, 9 and 13. Clap on 5 and 13.
  3. Open hat on 3, 7, 11 and 15. Those are the off-beats.
  4. Play it. Say it with the beat: boom-tss, boom-tss.

Polyrhythm: 3 against 4

Make a part 12 boxes long. Four even kicks and three even bells play at the same time.

On the machine
  1. Set the length to 12.
  2. Kick on boxes 1, 4, 7 and 10. That is four groups of 3.
  3. Bell on boxes 1, 5 and 9. That is three groups of 4.
  4. Play it. Listen to the kick, then the bell, then both at once.

Lesson 16 of 17

Arrange a song with drops

Intro, verse, hook and bridge. Chain them in song mode, and take the kick out for a drop.

On the machine
  1. Build four parts: 1 intro, 2 verse, 3 hook, 4 bridge. Leave the kick out of one of them for a drop.
  2. Chain six or more parts in the track, for example 1-2-2-3-4-3.
  3. Press PLAY TRACK.
  4. While it plays, press MUTE and take out a drum for a live drop. Bring it back on the hook.

Lesson 17 of 17

Make it knock, then hand it in

Mix the kit so the kick hits hardest and the hats sit back. Send it to the Turntables. Name it and save it.

On the machine
  1. Full bench, sliders on MIX: make the kick the loudest drum. Bring the closed hat down to 60 or lower.
  2. Switch the sliders to DECAY and shape the kick's tail: short to punch, long to boom.
  3. Press TO THE DECKS. Your beat lands on the Turntables.
  4. Type a name for your beat in Lesson 17. Press SAVE and keep the file. Fill in this sheet and press FINISHED.
