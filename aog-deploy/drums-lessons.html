Producers rarely start from nothing. Pick a style, listen, then change it until it sounds like you.

Play it live: fingers, keys and rolls

A drum machine is an instrument. Play the pads in time, record what you play, then hold a pad for a roll.

On the machine
  1. Play two pads at the same moment: two fingers on a tablet or phone, or the A and J keys on a computer.
  2. Full bench: press REC, then RUN. Tap a pad in time. Your hits land in the grid.
  3. Turn Roll to 1/16. Hold a pad and listen. It repeats in time with the beat.
  4. With REC on and the beat running, hold the closed hat. Look at the grid: the roll is there.

Boom-bap swing

The 1990s sampler sound. A slow tempo, deep swing, and a snare that leans back a little. Kit E is made for it.

On the machine
  1. Pick kit E (boom bap) in Sounds. Set the tempo between 84 and 96. Set Swing to 58% or 63%.
  2. Kick on box 1 and one kick off the beat, like box 11. Snare on 5 and 13.
  3. Full bench: set TC to OFF. Press REC and RUN, and tap a snare just after the beat.
  4. Play it back. Nod your head. That lean is the boom-bap feel.

Trap hi-hats and rolls

A fast tempo, a half-time snare, and hat rolls. Kit F has the 808. Hold a hat with Roll on and it rolls by itself.

On the machine
  1. Pick kit F (808 trap) in Sounds. Set the tempo between 130 and 150. Put the snare or clap on box 9 only. That is half time.
  2. Closed hats on every other box, then four in a row somewhere for a roll.
  3. Turn Roll to 1/16 or 1/32. Full bench: press REC and RUN, then hold the closed hat. The roll lands in the grid. (Or set TC to 1/32 and tap a hat between two boxes.)
  4. Play the trap beat. A roll at 1/32, or a hat between two boxes, is a 32nd note.

1987 or 2026? How sound becomes numbers

A sampler stores sound as numbers. In 1987 it kept fewer numbers, and that is the crunch. Turn the Sound dial and hear it.

On the machine
  1. Pick or build a beat. Turn the Sound dial all the way to 1987. Play it.
  2. Turn the dial all the way to 2026. Play it again. Listen to the hats and the snare.
  3. Full bench: press TUNE and pull one drum's slider down. Set the dial to 1987 and tap that pad. Hear the crunch.
  4. Find the spot in the middle where your beat sounds best. Play it there.
