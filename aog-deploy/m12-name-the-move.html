Mathematics · Activity 12

Name the Move

A property is a legal move. Both sides are always worth the same — something just changed how it looks. First make the move with your own hands. Then say its name.

Watching one first is a strong way to start.

Problem 1 of 10 Make the move first, then name it.

1Make the move

Read the left side, then the right. One thing is different. Pick the move that turns one into the other — the pieces will make it in front of you.

2Name it

The same six names every time — that is the whole word wall. Match the move to its pattern.
