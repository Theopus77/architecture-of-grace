The Blueprint

You have met the three branches. This is the document that builds all three — seven articles, one opening promise, and a heavy door for changes. Three sets: the plan and its parts, the ideas it runs on, and how it grows.

Show me one first

This one is nobody's answer — it is only here to show the moves before you touch yours. Watching it is instruction, never a trial, and it counts toward nothing.

Which article creates Congress?
Find the plan's shape first: seven articles, and the first three build the branches in order — lawmakers, president, courts.
Congress makes the laws — the first job the plan does is build the lawmaker. So Congress lives in the FIRST article.
Article I. Hold the shape — I legislative, II executive, III judicial — and a whole family of test questions answers itself.

Now number 1 is yours. The hint button is there the whole time, and using it is part of the lesson.
