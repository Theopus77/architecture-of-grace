4Guitar 2: the wall of sound

Guitar · the burst and the biggest

Same chords, but strum down and up, fast and even, like rain. Players call it tremolo picking.

Arms tired? Strum on every beat instead. It still sounds huge.

7Find the melody by ear

Guitar · Solo

The melody is the part you could hum. Musicians learn it the same way: listen, hum, find the notes, one short phrase at a time.

The guitar tune that rings over the big parts lives in A major. The Major scale lights all seven of its notes.

Open Slow listening: loop a phrase and slow it down

Open the Guitar

Stuck? Slow down: hum just two notes, then add one more. Every musician learns songs this way.
