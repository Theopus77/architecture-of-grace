Thinking-skills checklists

Short checklists that help students start, plan, focus and finish. Share one as a link, or print it.

How to use them

  1. Pick one skill to work on, not all of them.
  2. Do it together first. Say each step out loud.
  3. Then the student uses it alone, on screen or on paper.
  4. Look at the steps they skip. That is where to help.

Each step a student ticks draws one more part of a picture. The weekly self-check shows up as a chart on your Dashboard.
