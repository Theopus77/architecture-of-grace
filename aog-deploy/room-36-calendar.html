Before the year starts

  • Send the family letter at least 10 days before U1·L1. Letters and consent
  • Book the counselor: in the room for U2·L6 and U3·L6, on site for U3·L3, reachable the same day for U4·L3, U4·L5 and U4·L6.
  • Post the crisis lines where students can see them, all year: 988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline (call or text 988); Crisis Text Line: text HOME to 741741. Safety
  • Plan the start-of-year reflection. Students take the same one again in U4·L7. Check-ins and surveys
