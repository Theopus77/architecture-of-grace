Helpful Hints

🔔 Rounding: underline, then look next door

Underline the digit in the place you are rounding to. Look at the digit right AFTER it: 5 or more → round up; 4 or less → it stays. Then drop the rest. Tenths = 1st decimal digit, hundredths = 2nd.

Five or more, raise the score. Four or less, let it rest.

🚦 Order of operations (PEMDAS)

Parentheses → exponents (5² = 5 × 5 = 25) → multiply and divide left to right → add and subtract left to right. One step per line; bring everything else down.

5² is 5 × 5 = 25 — never 5 × 2.

📐 Decimals: three moves

Add/subtract: LINE UP the decimal points, fill gaps with zeros (48.7 → 48.70), bring the point straight down. Multiply: multiply like normal, then count the decimal places in the problem — the answer gets that many. Divide: move the decimal in BOTH numbers until you divide by a whole number (3.72 ÷ 0.8 → 37.2 ÷ 8).

Points line up to add. Places get counted to multiply. Points travel together to divide.

🧮 GCF and LCM

GCF = Greatest Common FACTOR: list the factors of each number (what divides IN evenly), circle the biggest one on both lists. LCM = Least Common MULTIPLE: skip-count each number, circle the first one both lists share. GCF of 8 and 12 is 4; LCM of 4 and 6 is 12.

Factors divide in. Multiples march out.

🔁 Mixed number ↔ improper fraction

Mixed → improper: (whole × bottom) + top, keep the same bottom. 7 1/8 → (7×8)+1 = 57/8. Improper → mixed: divide top ÷ bottom; the remainder rides over the SAME bottom. 38/5 → 7 R3 → 7 3/5.

The bottom number never changes teams.

🍕 Fractions: add and subtract

Same bottoms? Add or subtract the TOPS, keep the bottom: 3/7 + 2/7 = 5/7. Different bottoms? Find the LCM of the bottoms first, make equivalent fractions, THEN add or subtract. 3/5 + 1/6 → 18/30 + 5/30 = 23/30. Simplify at the end.

You can only add pieces that are the same size.

✖️ Fractions: multiply and divide

Multiply: straight across — top × top, bottom × bottom, then simplify. Divide: KEEP the first fraction, CHANGE ÷ to ×, FLIP the second — then multiply across. Mixed numbers: change them to improper fractions FIRST, every time.

Keep — Change — Flip. Only the SECOND fraction flips.

🗺️ The coordinate plane

A point is (x, y): the x number walks RIGHT (+) or LEFT (−) first; the y number then goes UP (+) or DOWN (−). The x-axis lies flat; the y-axis stands tall. Quadrants use Roman numerals and go COUNTERCLOCKWISE from the top right: I, II, III, IV. Points on an axis — like (0, 4) or (−5, 0) — belong to NO quadrant.

Run before you jump: x first, then y.

⚠️ The error gallery — traps with names

  • The sleepy neighbor: rounding 18.748 to 18.74? The 8 next door says round UP: 18.75.
  • Adding the bottoms: 3/7 + 2/7 is 5/7, never 5/14 — the bottom names the piece size, and the pieces didn’t change size.
  • Dividing without the flip: 3/8 ÷ 7/8 = 3/8 × 8/7 = 3/7. No flip, no dice.
  • Mixed numbers head-on: 3 1/4 − 2 1/2 attacked in pieces gives 1 1/4 — wrong. Improper first: 13/4 − 10/4 = 3/4.
  • The backwards point: (3, −2) is 3 right and 2 down — never 3 down and 2 right. x runs first.
  • Clockwise quadrants: I is top right, then COUNTERclockwise — II top left, III bottom left, IV bottom right.
  • GCF/LCM swap: GCF of 6 and 8 is 2 (a factor, small); LCM is 24 (a multiple, big). If your GCF is bigger than both numbers, you found a multiple.

🎯 How the test will ask

  • “Simplify if necessary” means the simplify step is PART of the answer — 26/4 isn’t done until it’s 6 1/2.
  • Rounding names the place in words — underline that place before you look anywhere else.
  • Plot-the-point questions grade the LABEL too — write the letter next to your dot.
  • Check division with multiplication: 3.72 ÷ 0.8 = 4.65 because 4.65 × 0.8 = 3.72.

✅ Before the test, can you…

  • Round 18.748 to the nearest hundredth AND 4.703 to the nearest tenth?
  • Do 5² before you add?
  • Line up 48.7 + 6.23 with a zero?
  • Tell GCF from LCM without peeking?
  • Turn 7 1/8 into 57/8 and 38/5 into 7 3/5?
  • Keep–Change–Flip a division of fractions?
  • Plot (3, −2) — right first, then down?
