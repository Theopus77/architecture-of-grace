1A song in two taps

Pick a pattern. Press Play. That is a song.

1 Pick a patternPop is a good first one.
2 Press PlayThe orange button.
3 Watch the keysOrange keys are playing. Pale keys fit the chord.
4 Watch the padsThe red ring shows the chord now.

2Find C

Black keys come in groups of two and three. C is just left of the two.

3Soft, loud, and the pedal

Hold notes · the pedalOn: notes keep ringing after you let go. Off: they stop. On a computer, hold Shift.

4A chord with your hand

Play one, skip one, play one, skip one, play one.

5The six pads, in any key

The numbers are steps of the scale. They mean the same thing in every key.

  • 1Home. Songs often start and end here.
  • 2Moody. Leads on to 5.
  • 3Moody, soft.
  • 4Bright. Away from home.
  • 5Bright. Wants to go home: tap 5, then 1.
  • 6Moody. The sad friend of 1.

In Minor the pads become 1, 3, 4, 5, 6 and 7, and the moody chords lead.

6The chord wheel

Every major chord goes round the outside, its minor just inside. Tap any chord to hear it.

1 Your six padsThey sit together, with their numbers. Gold ring: home.
2 Turn the wheelOne step changes the key. The pads follow.
3 Watch it playThe chord playing now is orange.

With Make my own on, a tap on the wheel adds that chord to your pattern. Any of the 24 can join.

7Make your own pattern

1 Clear, then Make my ownClear empties the pattern.
2 Tap padsIn the order you like. Up to 8.
3 End on 1It sounds finished.
4 Press PlayYour pattern goes round.

834 sounds, eight groups

Same keys, different color. That color is called timbre.

1
PianosHammers hit strings. Grand, upright, honky-tonk, bright, soft felt, toy.
2
Electric pianosMetal tines, reeds or a computer chip. And the funky clavinet.
3
Organs and accordionSpinning wheels or air through pipes. Never fades.
4
Mallets and bellsCelesta, glockenspiel, vibraphone, marimba, steel drums, bells.
5
PluckedHarpsichord, harp, kalimba, music box.
6
Strings and voicesSoft strings, recorded. A choir.
7
Tape keyboardsStrings and flute on tape, for prog rock.
8
SynthsString synth, warm synth, synth brass, synth lead.

10On a computer

Each key shows its letter. The letters stay put when you move up or down.

  • A S D F G H J KWhite keys, C up to C
  • W E T Y U O PBlack keys
  • ShiftHold for the pedal
  • 1 – 6The six pads
  • Z · XLower · higher
  • SpacePlay and stop
