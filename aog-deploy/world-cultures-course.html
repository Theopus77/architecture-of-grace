For the teacher

The course studies how people live together around the world: families, customs and fairness for the youngest readers; the continents, rich and poor and kinds of government in the middle grades; social class through history in Europe, Asia and the Americas in 6–8; the political spectrum from left to right, extremes included, in 9–10; and comparison, propaganda and a capstone in 11–12. Every ideology is described with what its supporters and its critics say, and the course never tells a student which side to take. Every lesson ends with three checks answerable from its reading.
