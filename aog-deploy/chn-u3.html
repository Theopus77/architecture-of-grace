Family, Friends and Harmony

Values

This unit is about family, friends and getting along. Confucius taught about caring for parents. The Daodejing talks about water, soft and strong.

You will learn how many families get ready for Lunar New Year. You will learn about Qingming. You will meet yin and yang, two sides that fit together.

By the end, you can name ways families show respect. You can tell why water can be strong. You can explain harmony in your own words.

Respect at Home and the New Year

Family
Big questionHow do families show respect and love for one another?
The story

Sweeping Before the New Year

A girl cleans the whole house, and her grandmother tells her why.

It was the day before Lunar New Year. Mei held a broom. She swept the kitchen. She swept the hall. She even swept under the beds.

'Why do we clean so much today?' Mei asked. Her grandmother, Nai Nai, smiled. 'We sweep out the old year,' she said. 'Then the new year starts fresh.'

'And tomorrow,' Nai Nai said, 'we will not sweep at all. We keep the good luck in.' Mei laughed. She hung red paper on the door.

That night the family made dumplings together. Mei folded one carefully for Nai Nai. 'Thank you for teaching me,' she said. Nai Nai gave her a warm hug.

Talk about itWhat does your family do to get ready for a special day?
Section 1

Caring for Parents and Grandparents

5.1

What Is Xiao?

Main ideaXiao means caring for and respecting parents and grandparents, as Confucius taught.

Confucius taught about xiao. You say it like ’shyow.’ It means caring for your parents and grandparents. It means showing them respect.

Confucius said xiao is more than giving food. Even animals get fed. Xiao means caring with a respectful heart.

From the source
More Than Food
A student asked about caring for parents. The Master said: people think it means feeding them. But dogs and horses are fed too. Without respect, what is the difference?
Analects, book 2, passage 7, retold simply from James Legge's translation (1861) (paraphrased)

What does Confucius say xiao needs besides food?

Words to know
xiao
caring for and respecting parents and grandparents
Check yourself

Why: Xiao means caring for and respecting parents and grandparents.

2. What did Confucius say xiao is more than?

Why: In Analects 2.7 he says it is more than feeding them.

3. What does xiao need, as Confucius taught?

Why: He taught that respect makes the difference.
5.2

Let Them Know Where You Are

Main ideaConfucius taught that caring children tell their parents where they are going.

Confucius gave a simple idea. When your parents are alive, do not go far without telling them. Let them know where you are.

Why? Parents worry about their children. Telling them where you go shows you care. It is one small way to show xiao.

Why: Analects 4.19 says to let parents know where you go.

Why: Telling them shows you care about their worry.

3. This small act is one way to show what?

Why: It is one way to show xiao, care for parents.

Lunar New Year

5.3

Getting Ready

Main ideaBefore Lunar New Year, many families clean the house and hang red decorations.

Lunar New Year comes in winter, in January or February. It follows the moon’s calendar. Many families clean the house before it starts.

They hang red paper with good wishes. Red is a color for good luck in China. Many families also eat a big dinner together on New Year’s Eve.

On New Year’s Day, many children visit grandparents and elders. They say good wishes. Elders give them red envelopes with a little money inside.

Many families make dumplings together. The dumplings are shaped like old Chinese money. They stand for a good year ahead.

At New Year there are lion dances and dragon dances. Dancers move inside a big, bright lion costume. Drums and cymbals play loudly.

Section 3

Remembering Family

5.6

Qingming, Tomb-Sweeping Day

Main ideaAt Qingming in spring, many families visit graves to clean them and remember ancestors.

Qingming comes in early April. It is also called Tomb-Sweeping Day. Many families visit the graves of their ancestors. Ancestors are family members who lived before us.

Families clean the graves. They bring flowers and food. Some burn incense. They remember and honor the people in their family.

Why: Qingming comes in early April.

Respect at Home

Families in many places show respect at home. They greet elders. They help with chores. They listen when others speak.

Be Like Water

Harmony
Big questionHow can soft things be strong, and different things fit together?
The story

One Drop at a Time

A tiny drop of water falls on the same stone every day.

High on a cave roof, a drop of water formed. Drip! It fell onto a big gray stone below. The stone did not move. It was very hard.

The next day, another drop fell. Drip! Then another, and another. Every day, for years and years, drops fell on the same spot.

The stone was hard. The water was soft. But the water never stopped. Slowly, slowly, the stone began to change. No one could see it yet.

One day a child came into the cave with a flashlight. She looked at the stone. There was a small, round hole in it! The soft water had made it, one drop at a time.

Talk about itHow could something soft like water make a hole in hard stone?
Section 1

The Best Is Like Water

6.1

Water Helps Everything

Main ideaThe Daodejing says the best way to be is like water, which helps everything.

The Daodejing talks about water. It says the best way to be is like water. Water helps all living things. Plants and animals need it.

Water does not fight to be on top. It flows to low places. The Daodejing says this is like the Dao, the Way.

From the source
Like Water
The best way to be is like water. Water helps all things and does not fight. It goes to the low places others do not want.
Daodejing, chapter 8, retold simply from James Legge's translation (1891) (paraphrased)

What are two ways water helps living things?

Words to know
water
in the Daodejing, a picture of being soft, helpful and humble
Check yourself

1. What does the Daodejing say the best way to be is like?

Why: Daodejing chapter 8 says the best is like water.

2. Where does water flow?

Why: Water flows down to low places.

3. What does water do for living things?

Why: The Daodejing says water helps all things.
6.2

Soft Can Be Strong

Main ideaThe Daodejing says nothing is softer than water, yet it can wear away hard things.

Water is soft. You can put your hand in it. But the Daodejing says nothing is better at wearing away hard things.

There is a Chinese saying: dripping water wears through stone. It means small steps, done again and again, can do big things.

From the source
Soft and Strong
Nothing in the world is softer than water. Yet for wearing away hard, strong things, nothing does better.
Daodejing, chapter 78, retold simply from James Legge's translation (1891) (paraphrased)

How does the cave story show this idea?

Words to know
soft
easy to press or move; not hard
strong
able to do hard things or last a long time
Check yourself

1. What does the Daodejing say about water?

Why: Daodejing 78 says water is soft yet wears away hard things.

2. What does 'dripping water wears through stone' mean?

Why: The saying means steady small steps add up.

3. Which is soft?

Why: Water is soft; you can put your hand in it.
Section 2

Yin and Yang

6.3

Two Sides That Fit

Main ideaYin and yang are two sides that fit together, like night and day.

Chinese thinkers used two words: yin and yang. Yin is like the shady side of a hill. Yang is like the sunny side.

Yin and yang are different. But they fit together. You need both. Night and day make one whole day.

6.4

The Round Symbol

Main ideaA round symbol shows yin and yang as dark and light swirling together.

Many people know a round symbol for yin and yang. One half is dark. One half is light. They curl around each other.

Look closely. The dark half has a small light dot. The light half has a small dark dot. Each side holds a bit of the other.

Think it through
A Picture in Words

Why do you think each side has a dot of the other color?

Words to know
symbol
a picture that stands for an idea
Check yourself

Why: It shows dark and light curling together.

2. What is inside the dark half?

Why: The dark half holds a small light dot.

3. What is a symbol?

Why: A symbol is a picture that stands for an idea.
Section 3

Getting Along

6.5

Bamboo Bends

Main ideaBamboo bends in the wind and does not break, a favorite picture in Chinese art.

Bamboo is a tall plant. When the wind blows hard, it bends. When the wind stops, it stands up again.

Chinese painters and poets often praised bamboo. Many saw it as a picture of staying calm. It bends but does not break.

Think it through
A Picture in Words

Look at a bamboo grove in a storm. The wind howls. The tall stalks bend low. Leaves shake. Then the wind stops. One by one, the stalks stand tall again.

How is bending in the wind like staying calm?

Words to know
bamboo
a tall, hollow plant that bends in the wind
calm
quiet and steady inside
Check yourself

1. What does bamboo do in strong wind?

Why: Bamboo bends in the wind and stands up again.

2. Who often praised bamboo in China?

Why: Chinese painters and poets often praised bamboo.

3. What does calm mean?

Why: Calm means quiet and steady inside.
6.6

Harmony with Friends

Main ideaConfucius taught that good people get along with others without making everyone the same.

Harmony means getting along well. Think of a song. Many different notes play together. They sound good as one.

Confucius said a good person lives in harmony with others. But not everyone has to be the same. Different friends can fit together.

From the source
Harmony, Not Sameness
A good person gets along with others but does not make everyone the same. A small person makes everyone the same but does not get along.
Analects, book 13, passage 23, retold simply from James Legge's translation (1861) (paraphrased)

How can friends who are different still get along?

Words to know
harmony
getting along well, like different notes in one song
Check yourself

1. What does harmony mean?

Why: Harmony means getting along well together.

2. What did Confucius say about harmony?

Why: Analects 13.23 says harmony does not mean sameness.

3. How is harmony like a song?

Why: Many different notes can sound good as one.
