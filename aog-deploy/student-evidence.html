How this record is compiled — the case manager's spine

Nothing on this page is re-typed. The goal spine is compiled from the IEP document the case manager already holds — goals, benchmarks, and supports come from the IEP paperwork on the educator device, exactly as written there. The schedule comes from the same place, so electives and adult check-ins land against real periods. The evidence then attaches itself: probes, check-ins, exit slips, and work samples flow in from the instruments where they were made, each keeping its source. The case manager curates; the system never invents.

Student Voice

In Marcus's words

His words stay his words — never converted into a score, and never separated from what he chose to do with them.

Marcus decides what happens to what he says. Anything marked kept he chose to keep; anything shared with the team, he chose to share. Nothing on this page rates, scores, or reinterprets his words.
